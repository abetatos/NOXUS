Spec

Preliminary result
docs/preliminary-results.html (with figures). Pipeline: TROPOMI NO₂ → steel-facility footprint (5 km) → background ring → ERA5 meteo regress-out → deseasonalise → relative index → validate vs CREA BF operating rate, 2019–2026.
Finding
Honest near-null with a clear confounder. Raw levels: NO₂ negatively correlated with the BF rate (monthly r=−0.35) — a policy/retrofit-driven NO₂ decline decoupled from output (Li 2024). After year-over-year detrending the sign flips positive but weak (monthly r≈0.19, peak +0.29 at lag +6 mo), below the 0.5–0.75 literature bar (Kim 2023). Tighter footprint (5 km) beats 15 km; double-differencing erases the signal (Kim warning).

ERA5 source = Copernicus CDS (server-side AOI subset) — ARCO-ERA5 Zarr considered and rejected (chunk-1 downloads global fields, ~30–760 GB). Deseasonalisation default yoy (double-diff demoted; stl/none kept). Both recorded in decisions/architecture-decisions.md + spec Q3/Q4.

Acceptance tests

Given/When/Then. AT2, AT3 and AT4 are the load-bearing ones: this is the project's first task that reads multiple targets, so the anti-p-hacking guarantees and the paired estimator must be proven before any real target is scored.

Locations: tests/validation/test_scale_contrast.py, tests/validation/test_paired_corr.py.

AT1 — Targets load; cadence aggregated down, never interpolated REQ-001/002, EDGE-002

Given a synthetic weekly feature and a synthetic 10-day target with known gaps.

When the pairing is aligned.

Then both sides are aggregated to monthly, the realised n matches a hand-computed inner join, and cadence is recorded on the row.

And not: the target is never forward-filled, interpolated or resampled up to weekly; no synthesised observation may enter any correlation.

AT2 — No feature is refit against any target REQ-003, NFR-001, ERR-004

Given the scale-contrast module and every fitting entry point in noxus.signal monkeypatched to raise.

When a full contrast run executes over all features × targets.

Then the run completes; the feature accessor exposes no fit/tune/select entry point; features are read from the committed parquet files only.

And not: no target value may reach any feature-construction code path. This is the weakened form of the NOX-009 structural guarantee and it must be enforced by test, since it can no longer be enforced by signature.

AT3 — The paired-difference estimator recovers a planted contrast REQ-012

Given an AR(1) fixture with a common driver x, a weakly-linked y_a and a strongly-linked y_b, with the true correlations known by construction.

When paired_corr_difference(x, y_a, y_b) runs.

Then the returned CI brackets the true difference and excludes zero, and it is narrower than the interval implied by comparing the two marginal CIs.

And not: when y_a and y_b are constructed with equal correlation to x, the difference CI must contain zero — the estimator must not manufacture a contrast.

AT4 — BH correction covers every computed pairing REQ-011, NFR-002, EDGE-004

Given a run producing k pairings, of which some are refused for short overlap.

When BH is applied.

Then family_size == k including refused pairings; the family size is written to every row and printed in the findings note; dropping one pairing from the frame demonstrably changes the adjusted p-values.

And not: no pairing may be computed, inspected, and then excluded from the family — the specific failure this test exists to prevent.

AT5 — Lag reporting discipline REQ-013

Given a fixture whose cross-correlation peaks exactly at −max_lag with |r| below its per-lag bound.

When the lead-lag block runs.

Then peak_is_boundary is True, peak_significant is False, and the rendered findings text states that no lead or lag is identified.

And not: the text must never say the feature “leads” or “follows” the target on the basis of such a peak — the exact error corrected in the paper's Section 6.5.

AT6 — All four verdict branches fire from config REQ-020/021

Given hand-built frames encoding: (i) national robust-significant with difference CI excluding zero; (ii) the mirror; (iii) neither significant, difference CI containing zero, national |r| = 0.35; (iv) neither significant, national |r| = 0.05.

When the verdict function is applied.

Then it returns scale-confirmed, inverted, no-scale-effect, pipeline-suspect respectively; changing cfg.pipeline_suspect_abs_r moves the (iii)/(iv) boundary.

And not: the verdict must be a pure function of the frame and config — no branch may depend on which target “looks better”.

AT7 — Arm-B gate honours both outcomes REQ-030/031/032, ERR-003

Given the T1 probe stubbed to GO, then to NO-GO.

When the task runs.

Then GO ⇒ Arm-B rows present with target_scale = "province" and the family size increased; NO-GO ⇒ Arm A only, and the findings note records the unresolved provenance gap.

And not: on NO-GO no licence- or pay-gated source may be substituted, and the task must not silently proceed as though provenance had been addressed.

AT8 — Sub-period split and mandatory caveats EDGE-003/005/006, REQ-022

Given a completed run.

When the CSV and findings note are written.

Then rows exist for full, 2019-2021 and 2022-2026; the findings note contains the shared-publisher limit (REQ-022), the processor-version caveat (EDGE-005) and the 13%-of-national reading (EDGE-006).

And not: a national correlation may not be reported on the full record alone.

AT-ERR-1 — Error states ERR-001/002

Given (a) an absent benchmark_auxiliary.parquet; (b) a pairing with 20 common periods.

When the contrast runs.

Then (a) raises naming noxus ingest-benchmark; (b) emits a refused row with the reason and no estimate, while still counting toward family_size.

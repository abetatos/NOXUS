#FindingHandling
1Arm B unavailable. Every NBS data endpoint returns HTTP 403 from the site WAF while static assets on the same host serve normally — an access policy on the query API, not an outage. Recorded as a reproducible probe (data/derived/nbs_probe.txt). No paywalled substitute (REQ-031). Consequence: the shared-publisher provenance gap is NOT closed — all four Arm-A targets are CREA/WIND. The findings note and the paper must say so.
2The verdict rests on one target by design. REQ-021 keys on primary_national (weekly national BF rate, |r| = 0.094). The monthly crude-steel pairing reaches |r| = 0.222, above the 0.20 threshold. Rule applied as pre-registered; not re-keyed after the fact. Both numbers are in the CSV and the findings note, so a reader can see the sensitivity.
3The national correlations are strongly sub-period dependent and largely negative in 2019–2021 (−0.34 to −0.39), collapsing toward zero in 2022–2026. Exactly the shared-secular-trend confound EDGE-003 anticipated, and inseparable from the unharmonised processor-version sequence (EDGE-005). Reported in the findings note; the paper must not quote the full-record number alone.
4A pipeline-suspect verdict is easy to over-read. The feature is a Tangshan-AOI window (~13% of national steel), not national-coverage NO₂. The verdict text and the findings note both state explicitly that this does not establish a broken pipeline, and that the decisive test needs a province/nation-scale cube (spec Q3, out of scope).

Nothing is robust-significant anywhere in the family of 24 pairings. Widening the target from the cluster to the nation does not strengthen the coupling; if anything the national pairings are more negative, and the paired contrast is negative throughout.

What this means for the paper. The cheap test that would have corroborated the spatial explanation was run and did not. The "binding constraint is spatial" claim must be demoted from a conclusion the evidence selects to a hypothesis this arm failed to support. It is not established that the pipeline is broken — a cluster-scale window was never guaranteed to proxy national output — but the paper can no longer present the spatial reading as the explanation its own evidence picks out.

Summary

Most residual structure of the NO₂↔CREA relationship is already documented in docs/exploration-results.html (rolling-18-month correlation, period splits, the Simpson's-paradox "decoupling" diagnostic). One thread is un-closed: the rolling-18-month correlation of de-trended NO₂ against the blast-furnace (BF) operating rate peaked at r ≈ 0.73–0.76 around 2023-06 — clearing the 0.5–0.75 literature bar — and then collapsed through 2024. The full TROPOMI series is now complete (the 2023-06 gap was filled 2026-07-06, so June 2023 is 29/30 days), making this the moment to test the thread properly.

This feature has two deliverables:

  1. A public/free curtailment + air-quality-control calendar for Tangshan/Hebei steel: heating-season production restrictions, blue-sky / anti-smog campaigns, sudden shutdowns/restarts, and major public-holiday effects (Golden Week, Spring Festival, major-event blue-sky enforcement). This is the long-deferred NOX-003/004 follow-up (Q4 / Q6a) the decision log flags as NOT-YET-APPLIED. Its existence, coverage and date-granularity are the central data-feasibility risk and are gated by a T1 assembly spike.
  2. An event study around the 2023 regime, reusing the NOX-004 catalyst machinery on the now-completed series: is the 2023 coupling driven by a few large curtailment events (the signal is real but event-bound, not a continuous tracker), and was the 2024 collapse a change in curtailment regime or an accelerating intensity-decoupling (NOX-003.1)?

Honesty (carried, non-negotiable). Strict no-look-ahead (curtailment dates are exogenous/public; causal detection baselines; any lead/timeliness claim uses causal windows fixed before viewing outcomes). Multiple-testing transparency (event windows + test grid fixed before viewing outcomes; n_tests recorded; BH-FDR across the window×variant×threshold grid). Anti-p-hacking (no window/threshold/regime boundary selected against CREA). The rigorous NULL is a first-class, designed-for outcome: the 2023 peak may be a multiple-comparisons artefact of the rolling window (many overlapping 18-month windows were effectively scanned), and the report must be able to conclude exactly that as a valid finding (Morris & Zhang 2019).

Scope boundary. This task explains the NO₂↔CREA regime structure. The market linkage (abnormal returns, tradeable signal) is deferred to NOX-013/NOX-014 and is out of scope here (assumption A1). Pure analysis on the existing completed cube + benchmark plus a newly assembled public calendar — no new satellite acquisition.

Functional requirements

EARS format. IDs are stable references for tasks and tests.

Curtailment-calendar assembly (T1 gate + build)

REQ-001Before the calendar is built, the system shall run a calendar-assembly feasibility spike (T1) that probes the public/free candidate sources (MEE / Hebei DEE / Tangshan municipal notices, CREA / Custeel / Mysteel public reports, reputable news) and emits a coverage-and-provenance report stating, per candidate event: source, publication/effective date, date-granularity (day | week | month | season), direction, and a securely-dated confidence flag — and a go/no-go verdict (see EDGE-001) on whether an event-level calendar is assemblable or only the coarse floor.
REQ-002When assembling the calendar, the system shall produce a tangshan_hebei_curtailment.csv spanning the TROPOMI era (2019-01 → present) with, at minimum, the coarse floor: annual heating-season production-restriction windows, Golden Week and Spring Festival holiday windows, and any securely-dated blue-sky / anti-smog campaigns or sudden shutdowns/restarts — each as a discrete interval (start, end, direction ∈ {up,down}, cause).
REQ-003When recording each calendar event, the system shall attach provenance (source, citation/URL, granularity, confidence) as additional columns; an event that cannot be sourced shall be excluded or explicitly flagged, never fabricated (a curtailment hidden from public reporting becomes a documented gap, not an invented date).
REQ-004When the calendar is consumed downstream, the system shall feed it through the existing noxus.catalyst.groundtruth.load_curtailment_calendar contract (columns start,end,direction,cause) unchanged; the extra provenance columns are tolerated and ignored by the loader (additive reuse, no change to NOX-004 code).

Regime identification (the 2023 peak, the 2024 collapse)

REQ-010When characterising the regime, the system shall recompute the rolling-18-month (and a 52-week) correlation of the deseasonalised footprint NO₂ against the CREA BF operating rate on the completed series, reusing the existing validation pathway (noxus.validation.preprocess / leadlag) and deseason variants (harmonic-K1 method of record + the NOX-003.1 intensity residual), and shall locate the 2023 peak and the 2024 collapse.
REQ-011When segmenting into regimes, the system shall use a fixed, pre-registered rule (rolling-peak-centred windows + fixed boundaries, Q4), not a boundary tuned to maximise the correlation, and shall report each regime's correlation with autocorrelation-robust significance (effective-N + Newey-West + moving-block bootstrap CI + block-permutation p) reusing noxus.validation.robust unchanged.

Regime-conditioned event study

REQ-020When running the event study, the system shall detect NO₂ production events on the NOX-003.1 residual (reusing noxus.catalyst.events.detect_events, causal baselines + coverage + ventilation/stagnation screen, unchanged), build the ground-truth production events from BF-rate jumps combined with the assembled curtailment calendar (reusing bf_rate_events + production_events), and match them within a fixed window per regime — reporting precision, recall, false-discovery rate and signed lead time (reusing match_events), not a Pearson r.
REQ-021When testing whether the 2023 coupling is event-bound, the system shall perform a leave-one-event-out / jackknife on the 2023 peak regime: recompute the regime correlation with each large curtailment event (and its surrounding window) removed, and quantify how much of the peak r is attributable to a few large events versus a broadly distributed continuous coupling.
REQ-022When attributing the 2024 collapse, the system shall report both competing diagnostics side by side: (a) a curtailment-regime change (the 2024 calendar's event set / cadence differs from 2022–2023) and (b) an accelerating intensity-decoupling (the NOX-003.1 secular-trend residual behaves differently in 2024), and shall not force a single verdict when both are consistent with the data (EDGE-006).

Honest-null & multiple-testing discipline

REQ-030When judging the 2023 peak, the system shall compute a rolling-window scan-maximum null: under a serial-dependence-preserving null (block-permutation / phase-randomisation of one series reusing robust.py block tooling), the distribution of the maximum rolling-18-month correlation attainable across the many overlapping windows, and shall report the observed peak's percentile against that null — i.e. whether r ≈ 0.76 is surprising given how many windows were effectively scanned.
REQ-031The system shall fix the test grid (deseason variants × match/rolling windows × event-magnitude thresholds × regime segments) before viewing outcomes, record the number of tests n_tests, and apply a Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction across the grid (reusing bh_fdr), so no single favourable cell is reported as significant without multiplicity control.
REQ-032The system shall enforce anti-p-hacking: no window, threshold, deseason variant or regime boundary is selected by maximising agreement with CREA; and the report shall be able to state the NULL conclusion — "the 2023 peak is consistent with a multiple-comparisons artefact of the rolling window" — as a valid, first-class outcome, not a failure (Morris & Zhang 2019).

No-look-ahead

REQ-040The system shall enforce strict no-look-ahead: NO₂ event-detection baselines/thresholds at time t use only data up to t (reused causal detector); curtailment dates are exogenous/public; and any lead-vs-publication claim (NO₂ event precedes the official BF-rate print) uses causal windows fixed before viewing outcomes, verified by a guard test (reusing the NOX-004 causal guard).

Reporting & CLI

REQ-050When the pipeline completes, the system shall emit a machine-readable regime table + event-study table + scan-max-null / FDR summary and a plain-language findings note that states the verdict (event-bound coupling | continuous coupling | multiple-comparisons artefact | inconclusive) with CIs and the multiplicity count, and shall fold a section into docs/exploration-results.html closing the thread.
REQ-051When the user runs the study, the system shall expose it reproducibly as noxus subcommand(s) (calendar assembly incl. a --probe T1 mode, and the regime study) driven by a recorded config (rolling window, deseason variants, match windows, thresholds, regime rule, bootstrap draws, FDR α, seed, paths); existing NOX-001..011 subcommands and defaults are unchanged.

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001Reproducibility. Every result reproduces from the committed NO₂ residual + CREA benchmark + the curated calendar CSV + recorded config; no live fetch at analysis time; bootstrap/permutation use fixed seeds.
NFR-002Public/free data only. Calendar sources are public/free and cited per event; no paywalled dataset without explicit developer sign-off (Q1).
NFR-003No look-ahead / causal. Detection and any lead claim use only information available at the decision time (incl. TROPOMI processing latency); curtailment dates are exogenous.
NFR-004Honest reporting & multiplicity. Precision/recall/lead + regime r with autocorrelation-robust CIs; n_tests and BH-FDR reported; the null (incl. the scan-max artefact conclusion) is first-class; no cherry-picked window/variant.
NFR-005Anti-p-hacking. No window/threshold/variant/regime-boundary is selected against CREA; the regime-identification and null tests take the benchmark as an outcome to be judged, never as a tuning target.
NFR-006No-publish. NO₂-derived regime/event series and any bulk raw calendar scrapes are gitignored; the small curated, source-cited calendar CSV + the small stats tables + figures may be committed (A2/Q2).
NFR-007Additive & stack conformance. Existing catalyst / validation / signal / CLI contracts are unchanged; new code is opt-in; Python ≥3.12; passes uv run pytest + uv run ruff check .; no new dependency (numpy/pandas/scipy/statsmodels + the existing catalyst/validation modules suffice).

Edge cases

EDGE-001Coarse-only calendar. If T1 finds only the coarse floor is securely datable (no rich event-level dates), the study runs on heating-season edges + holidays + any securely-dated campaigns, the event-bound test (REQ-021) is reported at coarse granularity with reduced power, and the report labels it so — the task still proceeds (safe default, Q1).
EDGE-002A heating-season blanket curtailment (long interval) is matched on its start/end edges, not every period inside, to avoid inflating recall (reuses the NOX-004 _calendar_edge_events behaviour).
EDGE-003A regime segment has too few events for a stable precision/recall or jackknife → the segment is labelled low-power and its metrics are reported with wide CIs, not silently omitted (cf. ERR-003).
EDGE-004A curtailment period is cloud-gapped (low NO₂ coverage) → not detectable as an NO₂ event; a true curtailment hidden by cloud becomes a reported missed detection (honest recall hit), not a silently absorbed false negative.
EDGE-005Overlapping rolling windows. The rolling-18-month scan produces highly autocorrelated, overlapping window correlations → the scan-maximum null (REQ-030) accounts for the effective number of independent windows; the naïve "one window hit 0.76" claim is never reported without this null.
EDGE-006Ambiguous 2024 collapse. Both diagnostics (regime change + intensity-decoupling) are consistent with the collapse → the report presents both and states the attribution is not identifiable from this data, rather than forcing a single cause (REQ-022).

Error states

ERR-001If the NOX-003.1 residual or the NOX-001 benchmark is missing, the system fails with an actionable message naming the producing command.
ERR-002If the curtailment calendar CSV is malformed (missing start/end/direction), the loader raises the existing actionable ValueError; if the calendar path is absent, the study degrades to BF-rate jumps only with a recorded note (reuses NOX-004 behaviour), rather than fabricating dates.
ERR-003If fewer than a configured minimum number of securely-dated events exist for the event-level jackknife, the system refuses the event-level test, falls back to the coarse-calendar study, and reports insufficient events (cf. EDGE-001/003), rather than emitting unstable rates.
ERR-004If a caller attempts to make the regime-identification / null estimator select a boundary or threshold by an explicit CREA-agreement objective, the system shall refuse (the estimators expose no such parameter); this is the runtime face of NFR-005.

Acceptance criteria

AC-001The T1 spike produces a coverage-and-provenance report and a go/no-go verdict (event-level vs coarse-floor), recorded before the build proceeds (REQ-001, EDGE-001).
AC-002The assembled calendar CSV carries start,end,direction,cause + provenance columns, loads cleanly through the unchanged load_curtailment_calendar, and every event is sourced or flagged (REQ-002/003/004).
AC-003On a planted-regime synthetic (a coupling switched on in one window, off elsewhere), the rolling-correlation identification recovers the peak window and the segmentation rule is order/seed-stable and not tuned to the benchmark (REQ-010/011, NFR-005).
AC-004On a planted event-bound fixture (a regime correlation created by a few large step events), the leave-one-event-out jackknife correctly attributes the peak to those events; on a planted continuous fixture it does not (REQ-021).
AC-005The regime-conditioned match reuses detect_events/production_events/match_events and reports precision/recall/FDR/lead per regime with CIs — not a Pearson r (REQ-020).
AC-006Scan-maximum null: on an autocorrelated AR(1) null fixture the scan-max-of-rolling-r test holds the false-positive rate near α (a genuinely-null series does not routinely produce a "significant" peak), while a planted-regime fixture's peak sits beyond the null band (REQ-030, EDGE-005).
AC-007Multiplicity: the test grid is fixed before outcomes, n_tests is recorded, and BH-FDR is applied across the grid; a favourable single cell that does not survive FDR is reported as non-significant (REQ-031, NFR-004).
AC-008No-look-ahead guard: a test proves detection at time t is unchanged by appending future data and that any lead-vs-publication window is causal (REQ-040, reuses the NOX-004 guard).
AC-009Honest-null path: when the scan-max null shows the peak is unsurprising and/or FDR wipes out the event matches, the report concludes "2023 peak consistent with a multiple-comparisons artefact" and this is treated as a valid PASS, not a failure (REQ-032, NFR-004).
AC-010The findings note + figures are produced, folded into docs/exploration-results.html; re-running with the same seed/config reproduces the tables identically; existing behaviour is unchanged; uv run pytest and uv run ruff check . pass (REQ-050, NFR-001/007).

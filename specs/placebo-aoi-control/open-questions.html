IDKindQuestionRecommendation / spec default
Q1blockingWhat transfer ceiling should trigger NO-GO at T1?Recommendation: the wide-AOI re-acquisition of NOX-015 is the natural yardstick — it moved 2,741 overpasses over a 1.25° buffer successfully. Propose a ceiling of eight times that volume, with NO-GO above it. Confirm or set a different figure before T1 runs.
Q2blockingShould placebos be matched on total NO2 loading as well as on cell count and latitude?Recommendation: no. Matching on loading would select placebos by a quantity downstream of the signal we are calibrating, which risks matching away the very contrast under test. Match on geometry only, and report loading per placebo as a covariate.
Q3non-blockingShould a positive-control placebo (a footprint over a different known large point source) be included?Recommendation: valuable but out of scope here; it overlaps NOX-017 and would be better as a follow-up once that task reports.

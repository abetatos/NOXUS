Requirements — Placebo AOI control (what does “cluster-local share ≈ 0” mean?)

Task ID NOX-018
Feature slug placebo-aoi-control
Status spec_ready
Author spec-author
Human approvalpending
Origin Paper critique B2 (2026-08-31): the study reports a near-zero share without an empirical null distribution to read it against.

Summary

The paper's load-bearing number is a cluster-local change-variance share of ≈ 0, and its ring sweep shows that number moves with the background geometry. What is missing is a scale for it: what share does this estimator return over a footprint with no blast furnaces at all? Without that, “≈ 0” and “0.061 at 90–150 km” are numbers without a null distribution. This task runs the identical pipeline over a set of matched placebo footprints elsewhere on the North China Plain that contain no BF facilities, producing the empirical distribution of the cluster-local share and of the benchmark correlation under a known-absent signal. It is the calibration that turns the paper's central statistic into an interpretable one. Unlike NOX-017 and NOX-020 it requires new acquisition, so it is gated on a feasibility probe.

Functional requirements

REQ-001The system shall select at least eight placebo footprints on the North China Plain, matched to the Tangshan footprint on cell count and approximate latitude, and verified to contain no facility from the committed steel-facility table nor any facility within one footprint radius of their boundary.
REQ-002Placebo selection shall be deterministic from a fixed seed and a declared exclusion mask, and shall be fixed before any NO2 data are read for the placebo AOIs.
REQ-003For each placebo footprint the system shall run the committed variance decomposition at the committed 25–60 km ring geometry and at 90–150 km, and shall score the resulting activity index against the CREA Tangshan benchmark under the same inference stack.
REQ-004The system shall report the empirical distribution — median, interquartile range, and the 5th and 95th percentiles — of the cluster-local share and of the weekly |r| across placebos, and shall locate the real Tangshan values within those distributions as percentile ranks.
REQ-005The system shall emit a two-way verdict fixed before the run: signal-indistinguishable when Tangshan's cluster-local share sits below the 95th percentile of the placebo distribution at both ring geometries; signal-elevated otherwise.
REQ-006Acquisition shall use the free openEO CDSE route only, and the task shall be gated behind a T1 feasibility probe reporting the transfer volume and wall-clock estimate before any bulk pull begins.

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001Free-data rule: no licence-gated or paywalled source, and no host outside the declared allowlist.
NFR-002The placebo cubes are gitignored like every other raw acquisition; only derived series and the results table are committed.
NFR-003The T1 probe must report an estimated transfer volume before the bulk pull, and the task stops if it exceeds a declared ceiling.

Edge cases

EDGE-001The North China Plain is densely industrialised; a “no BF facility” footprint is not a “no NO2 source” footprint. The control tests for a blast-furnace signal specifically, and every artefact and the paper text must state that limitation plainly rather than let the word “placebo” overclaim.
EDGE-002Placebo footprints must not overlap each other or the Tangshan footprint's background rings, or the draws are not independent; enforce a minimum separation and record it.
EDGE-003A placebo whose 90–150 km ring falls substantially over open sea is not comparable to Tangshan's; record the sea fraction per placebo and report the distribution both with and without the sea-dominated ones.

Error states

ERR-001If the T1 probe returns a transfer estimate above the declared ceiling, return NO-GO and stop, per the NOX-005 precedent.
ERR-002If placebo selection cannot find eight qualifying footprints under the exclusion mask, report how many it found and refuse rather than relaxing the mask — relaxing it after seeing the shortfall would make the selection outcome-dependent.
ERR-003If the verdict is signal-elevated, escalate: it would mean the Tangshan footprint carries something the placebos do not, which reframes the paper's null and must not be written up unattended.

Acceptance criteria

AC-001The placebo distribution and Tangshan's percentile rank within it are in the committed artefact at both ring geometries.
AC-002The selection mask, seed and separation rule are compiled-in constants introduced before the first placebo cube is read.
AC-003The paper reports the cluster-local share against its empirical null rather than against zero.
AC-004The “no BF facility ≠ no NO2 source” limitation appears in the paper text, not only in the code.

Requirements — Multiplicative (log-space) deseasonalisation and lifetime weighting

Task ID NOX-020
Feature slug multiplicative-lifetime-deseason
Status spec_ready
Author spec-author
Human approvalpending
Origin Paper critique B6 (2026-08-31); listed in the paper as outstanding control C6, the one identified mechanism that could have cancelled a real signal.

Summary

The tropospheric NO2 lifetime is roughly two to three times longer in winter than in summer, so the column-to-emission relationship is multiplicative and seasonal. Blast-furnace curtailment is also seasonal and concentrated in the heating season — precisely the season of maximum column sensitivity per unit emission. The paper's deseasonalisation is additive (an annual harmonic removed from the level, plus a linear meteorological control), and an additive removal does not remove an interaction between two seasonal cycles. If the harmonic fit absorbs the seasonal component of the response along with the seasonal component of the emissions, it suppresses exactly the signal the study is looking for, at the time of year it should be strongest. This is the one mechanism the paper identifies that could reverse its null, and it needs no new data.

Functional requirements

REQ-001When the multiplicative arm runs, the system shall recompute the weekly and monthly coupling between the NO2 activity index and the CREA benchmark with the deseasonalisation performed in log space, so that a seasonally varying multiplicative gain is removed rather than a seasonally varying additive offset.
REQ-002The system shall additionally compute a lifetime-weighted variant in which the column is divided by a seasonal lifetime factor before deseasonalisation, using a fixed published seasonal lifetime climatology declared in the config, not fitted to the data.
REQ-003Both variants shall be scored against the benchmark under the same robust-inference stack, the same coverage screen and the same equivalence-bound reporting as the additive arm, so that the additive and multiplicative results are directly comparable row for row.
REQ-004The system shall re-run the change-variance decomposition on the log-space basis, adding a third change basis alongside first-difference and deseasonalised, at the committed 25–60 km ring and at the 90–150 km ring.
REQ-005The system shall emit a two-way verdict fixed before the run: additive-artefact when a multiplicative variant raises the weekly |r| above the study's 0.17 exclusion bound with block-permutation p < 0.05 surviving BH-FDR across the variant family; null-robust otherwise.
REQ-006The system shall write data/derived/multiplicative_deseason.csv and a text summary, and shall report the additive baseline in the same table rather than in prose.

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001No new acquisition. Runs on the committed weekly and daily cubes.
NFR-002The seasonal lifetime climatology is a compiled-in constant with a literature citation in the config comment, fixed before the run.
NFR-003Seed 20260712, 5,000 draws, matching the rest of the study.

Edge cases

EDGE-001Log space is undefined for non-positive values. The background-corrected residual is signed and frequently negative, so the log transform applies to the footprint and ring columns separately, and the contrast is formed as a log ratio — that is the correct multiplicative analogue of the additive difference, and it must be implemented as such rather than by clipping.
EDGE-002Weeks whose footprint or ring column is non-positive after QA screening are refused with a reason and counted in the artefact, never clipped or imputed.
EDGE-003The lifetime climatology is monthly; weekly periods spanning a month boundary take the coverage-weighted mean of the two factors.

Error states

ERR-001If clipping or imputation would be required to take a logarithm, refuse the period and record it. Silent clipping would manufacture the very signal the arm is testing for.
ERR-002If the verdict is additive-artefact, stop and escalate: that outcome overturns the paper's central null and must not be written up unattended.

Acceptance criteria

AC-001The artefact carries the additive baseline, the log-space variant and the lifetime-weighted variant with identical inference columns, so the comparison is a table read rather than a prose claim.
AC-002The verdict rule is a compiled-in constant whose introducing commit predates the first scoring run.
AC-003The paper's outstanding-control C6 is replaced by a reported result with a verdict, in either direction.
AC-004The log-ratio contrast is unit-tested against a synthetic series with a known multiplicative seasonal gain, confirming that the additive estimator misses the injected signal and the multiplicative one recovers it.

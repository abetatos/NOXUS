Design — Multiplicative (log-space) deseasonalisation and lifetime weighting

Task ID NOX-020
Feature slug multiplicative-lifetime-deseason
Status spec_ready
Author spec-author
Human approvalpending
Origin Paper critique B6 (2026-08-31); listed in the paper as outstanding control C6, the one identified mechanism that could have cancelled a real signal.

Summary

The tropospheric NO2 lifetime is roughly two to three times longer in winter than in summer, so the column-to-emission relationship is multiplicative and seasonal. Blast-furnace curtailment is also seasonal and concentrated in the heating season — precisely the season of maximum column sensitivity per unit emission. The paper's deseasonalisation is additive (an annual harmonic removed from the level, plus a linear meteorological control), and an additive removal does not remove an interaction between two seasonal cycles. If the harmonic fit absorbs the seasonal component of the response along with the seasonal component of the emissions, it suppresses exactly the signal the study is looking for, at the time of year it should be strongest. This is the one mechanism the paper identifies that could reverse its null, and it needs no new data.

Approach

Placement

Extends noxus/signal/ with a deseason_mode of additive | log | lifetime rather than forking the estimator, so the three arms provably share the rest of the path. New CLI flag on the existing verb.

Why a log ratio, not a log difference

The additive contrast is footprint minus ring. Its multiplicative analogue is log(footprint) − log(ring) = log(footprint/ring), a dimensionless enhancement ratio. This is well-defined wherever both columns are positive and is the standard form in the plume literature.

The decisive test is the synthetic one

AC-004 is the requirement that gives this task its power. Injecting a known seasonal multiplicative gain into a synthetic series and confirming that the additive estimator loses it establishes that the concern is real before we ask whether it bit on the real data. Without that step a null-robust verdict would be uninterpretable.

Lifetime climatology

A fixed monthly factor, winter-to-summer ratio in the 2–3 range, declared in config with its source. It is deliberately not fitted: fitting a seasonal gain to data that also contains a seasonal target is how a spurious coupling would be manufactured.

External dependencies and freshness

Reuses the pinned dependency set in pyproject.toml; no version change is required by this task.

Risks

  • The verdict rule must be committed before the first scoring run, or the result is a chosen verdict rather than a pre-registered one — the defect NOX-015 recorded in its own first pass.
  • Any deviation from the committed estimator would make the comparison to the paper's published numbers invalid; the design imports rather than reimplements.

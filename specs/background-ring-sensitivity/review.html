Non-blocking findings, recorded

#FindingHandling
1REQ-031 is defective. The rule thresholds the share's LEVEL. AT3b shows a footprint-confined source (high share at every ring — evidence AGAINST self-subtraction) is misclassified as self-subtraction. C1 asks about the RISE. Recorded, not patched. Rewriting the rule after the run changed the real verdict from inconclusive to ring-robust — i.e. toward the answer that flatters the existing paper. Substituting a post-hoc rule for a pre-registered one is exactly what this project's design exists to prevent, so the pre-registered rule stands as the verdict of record and the rise is published as a labelled post-hoc diagnostic. A follow-up task should pre-register the rise formulation and re-run.
2Spec feasibility count was 7% optimistic. The 60–120 km ring holds 709 cells, not the 759 quoted in the spec: the scoping measurement omitted the AOI clip that background_ring applies. Corrected in requirements.html. Conclusion unchanged (still 2.5× the default ring).
3Outermost-ring selection bug, caught in review. Keying on ring_inner_km alone pooled (60,100) and (60,120), doubling the cell count the agreement rule counted against min_cells_agreeing. Fixed (_outermost keys on the inner/outer PAIR) and locked by a regression test. The verdict was inconclusive before and after.
4Footprint radius of record is 15 km, not the 5 km the NOX-009 spec prose and the paper both say. SignalConfig.footprint_radius_km = 15.0 (84 cells at 5 km vs 448 at 15 km). Out of scope for this task's code, but it is a factual error in the paper. Flagged for the documentation pass.

Result of record

Verdict: inconclusive. Neither reading is established.

  • Self-subtraction is not established. No cell rises past the pre-registered 0.05 materiality threshold (largest rise +0.031), and the two change bases disagree flatly: the deseason basis rises monotonically with ring radius (weekly 0.0064 → 0.0379) while first-difference stays clamped at zero and its raw incremental R² becomes more negative (−0.021 → −0.049). A ring simply eating the plume should hand variance back to both bases.
  • Ring-robustness is not established either. The share is demonstrably ring-DEPENDENT: it rises monotonically in the deseason cells and its bootstrap CI excludes zero at the outer rings, so the "≈0 within CI" claim NOX-009 makes at 25–60 km does not hold at 60–120 km. The regional share falls from ~0.94 to ~0.84–0.88 in every cell over the same sweep.
  • A limit the sweep cannot escape: the rise has not plateaued at the outermost ring available, and the cube reaches only ~124 km from the centroid, so we cannot show the ring ever fully clears the plume.

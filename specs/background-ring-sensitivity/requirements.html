Requirements — Background-ring radius sensitivity: is the cluster-local ≈ 0 share physics or ring self-subtraction?

Task IDNOX-015

Summary

Every cube-based result in this project measures the cluster signal as footprint minus a fixed 25–60 km background ring. The ring is assumed to carry regional background only. The NOX-005 flux probe measured a mean effective wind of |U| = 2.48 m/s under an assumed lifetime tau = 4 h, giving an advection length of |U|·tau ≈ 36 km — which lands in the middle of the ring.

If a material part of the cluster's own emitted NO₂ is present in the ring as advected plume, then subtracting the ring subtracts signal along with background. Worse, the NOX-009 partition enters terms in a fixed order (regional → meteo → local), so any variance shared between the ring and the cluster is booked to “regional” by construction. The headline result “cluster-local change-variance share ≈ 0” is therefore exactly what ring self-subtraction would also produce, and the paper's central claim is circular until this is tested.

This task sweeps the ring's inner/outer radii past the advection scale and re-runs the NOX-009 decomposition otherwise unchanged. The decomposition algorithm is not modified: only the ring geometry becomes a swept dimension. The outcome is binary and pre-committed:

  • Confirmed physical — the cluster-local share stays ≈ 0 at every ring radius, including rings entirely beyond the advection scale. Self-subtraction is ruled out and the paper's interpretation is supported.
  • Self-subtraction artefact — the cluster-local share rises materially (pre-registered threshold, REQ-031) as the inner radius moves past ~36 km. The NOX-009 headline is then partly a design artefact and must be restated in the paper.

The verdict is not asserted in advance. Both outcomes are first-class and reportable; a rise in the local share would be a correction of our own prior result, which is the point of running it.

Functional requirements

EARS format. IDs are stable references for tasks and tests.

Ring geometry as a swept dimension

REQ-001When running the sweep, the system shall treat the background-ring (inner_km, outer_km) pair as an explicit swept dimension, reusing noxus.attribution.source.background_ring unchanged (additive reuse — no edit to the ring-construction algorithm).
REQ-002The system shall sweep, at minimum, the fixed radius grid [(25,60), (40,80), (50,90), (60,100), (60,120)] km, which spans from the current default (straddling the advection scale) to rings lying entirely beyond it. The grid is fixed in configuration before any result is viewed (NFR-001).
REQ-003When a swept ring is constructed, the system shall record the advection scale |U|·tau (from the NOX-005 flux parameters, |U| = 2.48 m/s, tau = 4 h) alongside each ring, and shall flag each ring as straddles_advection / beyond_advection so the reader can see which rings are exposed to self-subtraction.
REQ-004If a requested ring yields zero cells after footprint exclusion and AOI clipping, the system shall record that cell as refused with its cell count and shall continue the sweep, rather than aborting (ERR-001).

Decomposition reuse

REQ-010For each swept ring, the system shall run the existing NOX-009 partition (noxus.signal.variance.decompose_from_levels) with every other parameter unchanged: same bases (first-diff, deseason), same resolutions (W, D), same meteo covariates, same entry order, same bootstrap draws and seed.
REQ-011The system shall emit one row per (ring × basis × resolution) cell carrying: ring radii, cell count, n, the four shares, the raw ΔR²_local (unclamped), the clamped cluster-local share, the sqrt(f) change ceiling, the block-bootstrap CI of the share, and the local_clamped flag.
REQ-012The system shall report every swept cell. No ring, basis or resolution may be dropped from the output for producing an inconvenient result (Morris & Zhang 2019; mirrors NOX-009 REQ-051).
REQ-013When the ring is azimuthally unbalanced, the system shall additionally compute the ring mean under equal-octant weighting (each of eight azimuthal octants contributing equally) and report the decomposition under both plain and octant-weighted ring means, so the far-ring anisotropy (see Feasibility) cannot be mistaken for a physical effect.

Pre-committed verdict rule

REQ-020The system shall compute, per (basis × resolution), the trend of the cluster-local share against ring inner radius, reported as the raw share at each radius plus the change from the 25–60 km default to the 60–120 km ring.
REQ-021The system shall report the change in the regional share across the same sweep, since ring self-subtraction predicts that variance moves out of the regional term and into the local term together.
REQ-022The system shall report, per ring, the azimuthal octant cell counts and the imbalance ratio (max/min octant), so a reader can judge whether a share change coincides with a geometry change.
REQ-030The system shall emit one of three verdicts, decided by the pre-registered rule in REQ-031 and by no other criterion: ring-robust (physics), self-subtraction (artefact), or inconclusive.
REQ-031DEFECT FOUND (AT3b, 2026-08-28; recorded, not patched): this rule thresholds the share's level, so a source that is genuinely footprint-confined — high share at every ring, which is evidence AGAINST self-subtraction — is misclassified as self-subtraction. C1 asks whether the share rises, so a future pre-registration should threshold the rise. It was NOT retro-fitted: on the real data the two formulations give different verdicts, so swapping them after the run would have replaced a pre-registered verdict with a chosen one. The rise is reported as a clearly-labelled post-hoc diagnostic instead (ring_dependence_diagnostic). Original text follows. The verdict rule, fixed before any result is viewed: self-subtraction if the clamped cluster-local share at the 60–120 km ring exceeds 0.05 and its bootstrap CI excludes zero, in at least two of the four (basis × resolution) cells; ring-robust if the share stays below 0.05 with a CI containing zero in all four cells at every ring beyond the advection scale; inconclusive otherwise. The 0.05 threshold is one order of magnitude below the regional share and roughly the level at which the implied change-detector ceiling sqrt(f) would reach ≈ 0.22 — the smallest local share that could plausibly matter to a nowcast.

Reporting

REQ-040The system shall write a tidy CSV (data/derived/ring_sensitivity.csv) and a plain-language findings note stating the verdict, the numbers behind it, and — if the verdict is self-subtraction — an explicit statement that the NOX-009 headline and the paper's Section 6.1 require correction.
REQ-041The system shall emit a figure showing the cluster-local and regional shares as a function of ring inner radius, with the 36 km advection scale marked, per basis × resolution.
REQ-042The system shall expose the sweep reproducibly as a noxus subcommand driven by a recorded config, following the NOX-008 scale-sweep and NOX-009 variance wiring. Existing subcommands and their defaults are unchanged.

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001Benchmark-blind. The estimator shall take no benchmark argument and the CREA series shall never be read anywhere in this sweep — enforced structurally, as in NOX-009. The verdict rule is fixed in configuration before results are viewed.
NFR-002Additive. No existing algorithm is modified. background_ring, footprint_mask, partition_change_variance and decompose_from_levels are reused read-only; the only change to existing code is threading a ring parameter that currently defaults to SignalConfig, preserving the present default exactly.
NFR-003Backward-compatible default. Running the existing noxus variance command with no new flags shall reproduce the committed variance_decomposition.csv byte-for-byte (regression, AT-REG-1).
NFR-004Deterministic. Fixed seed (reuse VarianceDecompConfig.seed = 20260706); repeated runs produce identical output.
NFR-005No new acquisition. The sweep shall run entirely on the committed cubes; if any requested ring needs data outside the cube it is refused (REQ-004/ERR-001), never silently truncated.

Edge cases

EDGE-001Ring exceeds cube extent. The cube reaches 124 km from the centroid, so a ring with outer_km > 124 is silently truncated by the AOI clip. The system shall record the realised maximum radius per ring, not the requested one, so a truncated ring is visible.
EDGE-002Azimuthal anisotropy grows with radius. Far rings over-weight the north/south lobes of the rectangular AOI. Handled by REQ-013 (octant weighting) and REQ-022 (reported imbalance), not by dropping the ring.
EDGE-003Ring/footprint overlap. Already handled by background_ring (footprint cells excluded); the sweep must not reintroduce overlap when the inner radius is small.
EDGE-004Negative raw ΔR²_local. Three of four NOX-009 cells already return a small negative raw incremental R². The sweep shall report the raw value and the clamped share at every ring (REQ-011); the verdict rule uses the clamped share, but a raw value that crosses from negative to clearly positive is itself reportable evidence.
EDGE-005Cell-count confound. Larger rings average more cells, mechanically reducing the ring series' own noise, which could shift the partition for reasons unrelated to the plume. The system shall report ring cell count per row (REQ-011) so this is auditable, and REQ-013's octant weighting provides a partially decoupled comparison.
EDGE-006Daily vs weekly divergence. If the verdict differs between cadences, the system shall report both and return inconclusive rather than selecting the cadence that agrees with the prior result.

Error states

ERR-001Empty ring. A ring with zero cells after exclusion/clipping is recorded as refused with its cell count; the sweep continues (REQ-004).
ERR-002Missing cube. Reuse the NOX-009 messages: weekly cube absent → “run noxus grid”; daily cube absent and daily requested → “run noxus grid --freq D”.
ERR-003Missing ERA5 snapshot. Fail early with the NOX-009 message rather than silently dropping the meteo term.
ERR-004Benchmark reachable from the estimator. Any code path that would let the CREA series into the sweep is a hard failure, verified by test (AT2).

Acceptance criteria

AC-001noxus ring-sweep runs end-to-end on the committed cubes and writes ring_sensitivity.csv with one row per (ring × basis × resolution × weighting), all cells reported.
AC-002The 25–60 km rows reproduce the committed NOX-009 numbers (regional 0.933–0.951, local 0.000/0.006, raw ΔR² −0.0209/+0.0064/−0.0264/−0.0003) to within floating-point tolerance.
AC-003A verdict of ring-robust, self-subtraction or inconclusive is emitted by the REQ-031 rule alone, with the driving numbers printed.
AC-004A synthetic fixture in which a known local source is present in the ring recovers a rising local share as the inner radius grows (positive control, AT3) — establishing that the sweep can detect self-subtraction when it exists.
AC-005Existing noxus variance output is unchanged (NFR-003); full test suite and ruff check pass.

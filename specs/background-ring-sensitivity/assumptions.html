Assumptions

Recorded so a reviewer can attack them directly. Each states what we assumed, why, what happens if it is wrong, and whether it blocks implementation.

A1 — The advection scale is |U|·tau ≈ 36 km

Assumed: the NOX-005 mean effective wind (2.48 m/s) and the assumed NO₂ lifetime (tau = 4 h) give the relevant plume length for deciding whether the 25–60 km ring is contaminated.

Why: both numbers are already measured/fixed in the flux probe; using them keeps this task consistent with the analysis it is auditing.

If wrong: tau is seasonal (2–3× longer in winter, see NOX critique C6), so 36 km may understate the winter plume length by a factor of 2–3. That would mean even the 60–120 km ring is partly contaminated in winter and the sweep would under-detect self-subtraction.

Mitigation: the sweep spans 25→120 km rather than testing a single alternative radius, so the trend — not one threshold — carries the answer. The advection scale is recorded as a marker, never used as a cut.

Blocks implementation: no.

A2 — The committed cube is adequate for a 60–120 km ring

Assumed: no new acquisition is needed.

Why: measured, not assumed — the cube reaches 124 km from the centroid and a 60–120 km ring holds 759 cells across all eight octants at 0.99 valid-week fraction (see Feasibility).

If wrong: n/a — verified read-only before the spec was written.

Blocks implementation: no.

A3 — A rising local share at larger radii means self-subtraction, not something else

Assumed: the causal reading of a positive result.

Why: if the near ring carries the plume, removing it removes signal; moving the ring out should restore it.

If wrong: a larger ring is also a worse proxy for the local background (different air mass, different sources, more cells and hence less ring noise). Any of these could move the partition without any plume being involved.

Mitigation: AT3b (negative control: source confined to the footprint ⇒ share must stay flat), EDGE-005 (cell counts reported), REQ-013 octant weighting, and REQ-021 (regional share must fall as the local share rises — the signature of a transfer between the two terms rather than a generic drift).

Blocks implementation: no — but it does mean a self-subtraction verdict must be reported as “the ≈0 result is not robust to ring geometry”, not as “the cluster signal is recoverable”.

A4 — 0.05 is the right pre-registered threshold

Assumed: a clamped local share above 0.05 with a CI excluding zero is materially different from “≈ 0”.

Why: one order of magnitude below the regional share, and sqrt(0.05) ≈ 0.22 — roughly the smallest change-detector ceiling that could plausibly matter to a nowcast.

If wrong: the threshold is a reporting convenience; the raw shares and CIs are published for every cell, so a reader can apply their own.

Blocks implementation: no. But it must be fixed before T7 runs and never adjusted afterwards.

A5 — The fixed partition order stays fixed

Assumed: the sweep varies the ring only, keeping regional → meteo → local.

Why: varying two things at once would make the result uninterpretable, and the order is the NOX-009 result of record.

If wrong: the order-dependence of the partition is a separate known limitation (paper §3, Sequential variance partition) and deserves its own task; it is out of scope here.

Blocks implementation: no.

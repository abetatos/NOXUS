Open questions — Wide-AOI ring sweep (NOX-017)

Open questions

#QuestionStatusResolution
Q1How wide is wide enough — where does the ring stop being a background and start being a different region?Decided, not resolved.Buffer 1.25° (ring to ~150 km). Beyond that the ring is Beijing and open sea, and “regional background” loses meaning. The limit is interpretive, not computational, and is stated as such.
Q2Should the wide run also re-do the NOX-009 headline decomposition, not just the sweep?Out of scope.NOX-009's headline is defined against the committed 25–60 km ring. The wide run's job is to say whether that headline is ring-dependent — it does not replace it.
Q3If Rule A and Rule B disagree, which is the verdict of record?Pre-registered.Neither. rules-disagree is itself the verdict and both numbers are reported. Choosing one after seeing the data is precisely the failure NOX-015 recorded.
Q4Does a rise that appears only past ~120 km support the paper's spatial reading?Open — interpretive.Partly at best. A rise driven by an outer ring that is a different air mass is not the same evidence as a rise driven by escaping the plume. Flagged for the paper; not resolvable by this run.
Q5Should the extra ~200 MB of wide raw data be preserved?Decided.Kept locally under gitignored data/raw/tropomi_wide/; never committed (NFR-004). Reproducible from the recorded config and the manifest.

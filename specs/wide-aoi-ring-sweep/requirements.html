Summary

NOX-015 swept the background ring from 25–60 km out to 60–120 km and returned inconclusive. Two things were left unresolved, and both are geometric:

  • The deseason-basis local share rose monotonically with ring radius (weekly 0.0064 → 0.0379, CI excluding zero at the outer radii) and had not plateaued at the outermost ring available. A rise that is still climbing when the domain runs out cannot be read either way.
  • The committed cube (AOI buffer 0.25°) reaches only 63 km from the cluster centroid with full 360° coverage; beyond that the “ring” is four corner wedges (corner maximum ≈ 126 km). The 60–120 km ring was therefore not an annulus at all, which the equal-octant weighting mitigates but does not repair.

This task acquires a wider AOI cube so a genuine annulus exists out to ~150 km, and re-runs the same partition over an extended ring grid. It also repairs, by pre-registration rather than post-hoc, the NOX-015 verdict-rule defect recorded in that task's review: the committed rule thresholded the level of the local share instead of its rise, so a source confined to the footprint — evidence against self-subtraction — would have been classified as self-subtraction.

Every threshold below is fixed before the wide cube is acquired or any wide-ring number is viewed. NOX-015's rule is carried over unchanged and reported alongside the corrected one, so the two are comparable and neither is chosen after the fact.

Functional requirements

Acquisition and gridding

REQ-001 Acquire TROPOMI NO₂ over the Tangshan AOI at buffer 1.25° for the full record (2019-01-01 → today), into a separate raw store, leaving data/raw/tropomi/ untouched.

REQ-002 The acquisition must be resumable and idempotent (manifest-backed), and must not require re-downloading on re-run.

REQ-003 Composite the wide store into weekly and daily cubes with the same gridding configuration as the committed cubes.

REQ-004 The temporal masking rule must be held identical to the committed cube. period_coverage is currently the valid-cell fraction over the whole box, so a wider box would mask a different set of periods and change n — a second knob moving at the same time as the ring. Coverage must therefore be evaluated over the committed 0.25° AOI sub-box of the wide cube.

REQ-005 The retained period set of the wide weekly cube must be compared against the committed cube and any difference reported, not silently absorbed.

Ring geometry

REQ-010 background_ring clips the ring to an AOI box that currently defaults to the 0.25°-buffer TANGSHAN region. The sweep must pass the wide AOI explicitly, or the wide rings would be silently clipped back to the narrow box and the whole exercise would be a no-op.

REQ-011 The footprint definition (15 km around the 26 active facilities), the meteo covariates, the deseason method, the entry order regional → meteo → local, the bootstrap and the seed are inherited unchanged from NOX-015. The ring is the only knob that moves.

REQ-012 Extended pre-registered ring grid, in km: (25,60) (40,80) (50,90) (60,100) (60,120) (75,135) (90,150). The first five reproduce NOX-015 exactly so the two runs are directly comparable; the last two are new and lie entirely beyond the domain the old cube could support.

REQ-013 For every ring, report realised cell count, populated octants, octant imbalance and the realised minimum/maximum radius, so a ring that is still geometrically incomplete is visible rather than assumed.

Pre-registered verdict rules

REQ-020 Rule A (LEVEL — carried over from NOX-015, unchanged). Self-subtraction if the clamped local share exceeds 0.05 with a bootstrap CI excluding zero in at least 2 of the (basis × resolution) cells at the outermost ring. Reported for comparability with NOX-015; its known defect is documented, not repaired in place.

REQ-021 Rule B (RISE — the corrected formulation, pre-registered here for the first time). Self-subtraction if share(outermost) − share(25–60 baseline) > 0.05 in at least 2 cells and the outermost cell's bootstrap CI excludes zero. This thresholds the quantity self-subtraction actually predicts.

REQ-022 Plateau criterion — the reason the wider cube was acquired. The rise has plateaued if the share increment between the two outermost rings is < 0.01 in every measurable cell. A verdict that depends on a rise which has not plateaued must be reported as domain-limited, whatever its sign.

REQ-022 revision, disclosed. As first written this was a quorum — “flat in at least min_cells_agreeing cells”. A pilot run on 8 of the 89 acquired months, executed to smoke-test the wide code path, showed the quorum to be trivially satisfiable: the first-difference basis clamps at exactly 0.0 at every ring, so its increment is zero by construction, and two such censored cells met the quorum while the deseason cells were still climbing by +0.16. The quorum reading also contradicts the plain meaning of the word: if any cell is still moving, the share has not plateaued. The rule was therefore revised before the full-record run and before any full-record verdict existed. Two things are stated plainly rather than buried: the pilot used real (partial) data, not a synthetic fixture; and the revision makes domain-limited more reachable — it moves the verdict away from the answer that would flatter the paper, not toward it.

REQ-023 The verdict of record is the conjunction of Rules A and B plus the plateau flag, stated explicitly. Rules A and B disagreeing is a reportable outcome, not an error to resolve by picking one.

REQ-024 No threshold in REQ-020..022 may be altered after any wide-ring number is viewed. If a rule is found defective mid-run, the defect is recorded and the pre-registered rule still carries the verdict — exactly as in NOX-015.

Reporting

REQ-030 Emit a machine-readable CSV of every (ring × weighting × basis × resolution) cell and a human-readable findings note carrying the verdict, the two rules, the plateau flag and the geometry audit.

REQ-031 Update the paper's §6.3 with the wide-cube result, stating plainly whether it supersedes, confirms or fails to resolve the NOX-015 inconclusive verdict.

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001 Benchmark-blind. The CREA benchmark is never read anywhere in the acquisition, gridding or sweep path.

NFR-002 Pre-registration. All thresholds fixed in configuration before the run; defects recorded, never retro-patched.

NFR-003 No regression. The committed noxus variance and noxus ring-sweep outputs must stay byte-identical on their default paths. New behaviour is reachable only via explicit new arguments.

NFR-004 Repository hygiene. The wide raw store and wide cubes live under gitignored paths; nothing large is committed.

NFR-005 Credentials are referenced by environment-variable name only and never read, printed or logged.

Edge cases and error handling

EDGE-001 A ring whose octants are not all populated is still reported, flagged as geometrically incomplete.

EDGE-002 The wide box extends over the Bohai Sea and toward Tianjin; sea cells and a second urban plume enter the outer rings. This is a property of the test, not a bug — but it must be stated, because at 150 km the “regional background” is no longer the same air mass.

EDGE-003 Batches that fail mid-acquisition are recorded in the manifest and retried; a partially acquired record must be detected and refused rather than analysed.

EDGE-004 A negative raw ΔR² for the local term clamps to zero, as in NOX-009/015, and the clamp is flagged.

ERR-001 Missing wide cube ⇒ actionable message naming the command that builds it.

ERR-002 If the acquisition cannot complete, the task stops and escalates; a truncated record is never presented as the wide result.

Findings recorded during the run

F1 — The plateau rule was defective and was revised before the full run

A pilot on 8 of the 89 months, run to smoke-test the wide code path, showed the quorum form of the plateau criterion to be trivially satisfiable: the first-difference basis clamps at exactly 0.0 at every ring, so its increment is zero by construction, and two such censored cells declared a plateau while the deseason cells were still climbing by +0.16. The rule now requires every measurable cell to be flat.

Two disclosures, because both matter to whether the pre-registration is still worth anything. The pilot used real partial data, not a synthetic fixture. And the revision makes domain-limited more reachable — it moves the verdict away from the answer that would flatter the paper, which is the opposite direction from the temptation NOX-015 recorded. The full-record verdict did not exist when it was made. Covered by a regression test.

F2 — Spec assumptions A2 and A3 were false as written, and were corrected

The wide grid's step matches the committed one but its origin does not: the box starts exactly 1.0° away, which is not an integer multiple of either step. The footprint therefore discretises to 447 cells rather than 448, and the ERA5 footprint means shift by up to 0.048 m s⁻¹ (11.9 m BLH) over 389 common weeks. Handled empirically rather than by assumption: re-running the committed 25–60 km ring on the wide cube truncated to the original record's end reproduces every share to within 0.0035 and every sample size to within 2 observations. The offset is immaterial and the wide/narrow comparison stands.

F3 — The NOX-015 sweep's outer rings were not annuli

Confirmed and quantified: the 0.25° AOI reaches only 63 km from the cluster centroid with full 360° coverage. Its 60–120 km "ring" was four corner wedges holding 709 cells; the same ring on the wide cube is a complete annulus of 1,737. This is why the two runs report different numbers at nominally identical radii beyond 63 km, and the paper now says so.

F4 — What the wide run adds, stated as a negative

Quadrupling the AOI area and pushing the ring to 150 km did not produce convergence. The ring radius is not a parameter that settles down over any domain this cluster's geography allows. Beyond ~120 km the ring spans the Bohai Sea and the Tianjin plume, so a still-wider cube would confound the test rather than settle it — the limit is now physical, not computational.

Follow-ups (recorded, not requested work)

  • The ring sweep is closed as far as this geography allows. A still-wider cube is not recommended: past ~120 km the ring is a different air mass.
  • Separating "the share rises because the ring escapes the plume" from "the share rises because the ring changes air mass" would need a directional or sectoral background rather than a concentric one — a different design, not a wider domain.

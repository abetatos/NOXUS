Assumptions — Wide-AOI ring sweep (NOX-017)

Assumptions

#AssumptionBasisIf wrong
A1CDSE openEO serves the 3.58° × 3.76° box for the whole record at the rate measured on one month.Measured live 2026-08-28: 81.4 s, 30 overpasses, 2.2 MB for 2023-06.Acquisition stalls; manifest resumes; a partial record is refused (ERR-002).
A2The wide grid keeps the committed step, so the footprint sample is unchanged. PARTLY FALSE — measured, and corrected here. The step is identical (0.05456° × 0.03472°) but the grid origin is not: the wide box starts exactly 1.0° west and north of the committed one, and 1.0° is not an integer multiple of either step, so the two grids are offset by a fraction of a cell.Measured on a pilot cube: the footprint discretises to 447 cells instead of 448 (0.2%).The run is no longer literally “one knob moved”. Handled empirically, not by assumption: the sweep's first ring is the committed 25–60 km ring, so the wide-cube baseline row is compared directly against the committed narrow result. A baseline that reproduces makes the sub-cell offset immaterial; one that does not is reported as a confound.
A3The meteo term is identical because the footprint is far inside the ERA5 box. NEARLY true — measured. ERA5 need not be re-acquired, but the nearest-neighbour regrid follows the offset grid, so the footprint-mean covariates differ slightly.Measured over 389 common weeks: max |Δ| = 0.038 m s⁻¹ (u10), 0.035 (v10), 0.048 (wind speed), 11.9 m (BLH).Differences are far below the covariates' own variability, but they are recorded rather than described as zero, and they fold into the same baseline-row check as A2.
A4Holding the temporal mask on the committed sub-box makes n comparable between the narrow and wide runs.REQ-004 design; verified by comparing retained period sets (REQ-005).Reported as a difference rather than absorbed; the comparison is then caveated.
A5A ring at 90–150 km is still a meaningful “regional background” for this cluster.Weak. At that radius the ring spans the Bohai Sea and the Tianjin plume.This is the main interpretive limit and is stated in the findings note and the paper (EDGE-002), not assumed away.
A6The developer's instruction “un cubo más ancho de ~124 km haz esto y damos por cerrado todo” (2026-08-28) constitutes approval for this scoped follow-up.Explicit instruction, recorded verbatim in tasks.json under approval.If not intended as approval, the run is discarded; nothing is committed without a further request.

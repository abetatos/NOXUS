Assumptions

A1 Variance partition = sequential nested-model ΔR² (not commonality/Shapley) Pending
Assumption
The four-term budget (regional → +meteo → +steel-local → noise) is estimated as the incremental R² of a nested regression entered in that fixed order, rather than by a commonality analysis or Shapley/LMG variance decomposition that redistributes shared variance symmetrically.
Reason
Sequential ΔR² is transparent, dependency-free (numpy/statsmodels only, NFR-007), and auditable; the entry order is recorded (REQ-021) and the collinearity caveat is stated (EDGE-001). Shapley/LMG would need extra tooling and obscures which term "owns" shared variance.
Risk level
Medium
Impact if wrong
If regional/meteo/local variance is strongly collinear, the order flatters whichever term enters last-but-one; the steel-local share could be under- or over-stated. Mitigated by reporting the raw ΔR² per step and stating order-dependence; a Shapley variant can be added later without changing the interface.

A2 Nested entry order = regional → meteo → steel-local Pending
Assumption
Terms enter in the order regional common-mode first, then meteo, then the steel-local footprint residual — i.e. the steel-local share is what remains after the confounders that the project already treats as nuisance (BTH background, ERA5 meteo) are removed.
Reason
This is the conservative order for the headline quantity: it credits shared variance to the confounders, so the steel-local share is a lower bound, consistent with the project's honest-null discipline (NFR-003).
Risk level
Low
Impact if wrong
A different order would raise the nominal steel-local share; since we deliberately want the conservative estimate, choosing this order cannot inflate the conclusion.

A4 Moving-block bootstrap block length reuses the NOX-008 rule Pending
Assumption
The autocorrelation-aware CI on the steel-local share (REQ-040) uses the moving-block bootstrap already in noxus/validation/robust.py, with the same block-length heuristic NOX-008 adopted (effective-N / first-order autocorrelation based), rather than a new block-length rule tuned for this task.
Reason
Consistency with the project's established significance tooling; avoids introducing a second, divergent autocorrelation convention.
Risk level
Medium
Impact if wrong
Block length that is too short understates the CI width; too long wastes power. Mitigated by reporting the block length used and the naïve i.i.d. interval alongside (REQ-040), so the reader sees the autocorrelation inflation explicitly.

A5 Meteo term reuses the existing linear ERA5 regression (not a non-linear model) Pending
Assumption
The meteo share (term b) is the variance explained by the existing linear ERA5 regress-out (u10, v10, blh, wind_speed), not a non-linear deweathering model.
Reason
Non-linear deweathering is the scope of NOX-010; NOX-009 measures the budget under the meteo treatment the pipeline uses today, so its verdict is about the current method. If NOX-009 finds a large "noise" term, that is exactly the evidence that motivates NOX-010 — keeping them separate avoids conflating "meteo under-removed by a linear form" with "genuinely unexplained".
Risk level
Medium
Impact if wrong
Some variance a non-linear model would attribute to meteo lands in the "noise" term, slightly deflating the meteo share and inflating noise. This is a designed hand-off to NOX-010, not an error; it is stated in the findings.

A6 Reported headline basis = deseasonalised residual at weekly resolution Pending
Assumption
All four (basis × resolution) budgets are computed and reported (REQ-011, NFR-003), but the headline ceiling quoted in the findings narrative is the deseasonalised-residual / weekly cell — the basis the validation chain actually correlates against CREA.
Reason
The weekly deseasonalised residual is the series behind the reported weekly r=0.07, so its change-variance ceiling is the one that directly reframes that null. The other three are reported for scale/cadence context, not cherry-picked away.

Only a narrative emphasis; every basis/resolution is in the table regardless (NFR-003 forbids hiding any).

A7 Daily basis uses the completed post-2026-07-06 cube (June-2023 gap filled) Pending
Assumption
The daily-resolution budgets are computed on the cube rebuilt after the 2026-07-06 fetch that filled the 2023-06-06..30 gap (TROPOMI now ~97.1% daily coverage), so gap-aware first differencing (EDGE-003) operates on the most complete series available.
Reason
The daily basis is the most gap-sensitive; using the completed series maximises effective n and avoids the truncated-June-2023 artefact.
Risk level
Low
Impact if wrong
If the daily cube is not rebuilt first, daily n is lower and the June-2023 window is under-represented; the fix is a noxus grid --freq D re-run before the decomposition (a task step).

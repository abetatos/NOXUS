Summary

The project currently justifies its signal-to-noise ceiling with an imported mean-level share: Wen (2024) reports steel ≈ 30–43% of Tangshan NO₂ concentration, and we argued a level-correlation ceiling of ≈ sqrt(0.40) ≈ 0.63. That is the wrong ceiling for a change detector. A steel-activity detector's signal lives in the covariance of changes, not the mean level — and the mean share was never measured on our data. The full-series result is a near-null (weekly Pearson r = 0.07, p = 0.18, N = 358; monthly ≈ 0.13); one regime shows a rolling-18-month r ≈ 0.76 at 2023-06; NOX-008 found 0/128 scale×variant×lag tests robust after FDR.

This feature empirically measures what fraction of the variance of changes in the meteo-normalised, deseasonalised footprint NO₂ signal is attributable to a budget of four terms, and converts that steel-local share into an implied SNR ceiling for a change detector, explicitly contrasted with the imported level ceiling. It is pure analysis on the existing analysis-ready cube (data/derived/no2/) — no new acquisition.

  • (a) Shared regional mode — the variance captured by the background ring (25–60 km) anomaly (the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei common mode).
  • (b) Meteo term — the variance explained by the existing ERA5 regression (u10, v10, blh, wind_speed).
  • (c) Steel-footprint-specific LOCAL residual — the incremental variance of the footprint (5 km) over the background+meteo model: the part that could carry an activity signal.
  • (d) Unexplained noise — the residual white/retrieval component.

The budget is computed on two change bases — first differences and the deseasonalised residual (NOX-003.1 intensity-model residual / harmonic-K1) — at weekly and daily (NOX-006) resolution. The deliverable is a variance-partition table plus the implied change-detector ceiling (≈ sqrt(local share)) reported against the level ceiling (≈ sqrt of the Wen cap). The interpretation reframes the near-null: if the steel-local change-variance share ≈ the level share, the null is SNR-bound (physical); if it is >> the level share, there is headroom and the null is method/target-bound — which motivates NOX-010 (deweathering) and NOX-011 (VIIRS triangulation).

Honesty (carried over): the decomposition is computed on NO₂ + meteo + background alone, never regressed against or tuned to the CREA benchmark (mirrors NOX-003.1's benchmark-blind discipline). An honest result is designed for: it may confirm the ceiling (null is physical) or reveal headroom (null is method-bound) — both are reportable products (Morris & Zhang 2019).

Functional requirements

EARS format. IDs are stable references for tasks and tests.

Signal construction (benchmark-blind)

REQ-001When given the analysis-ready cube, the system shall build the footprint (5 km) and background-ring (25–60 km) per-period series using the existing attribution pathway (noxus.attribution.source footprint mask + background ring; radii from SignalConfig), with no change to those algorithms (additive reuse).
REQ-002When constructing the analysis series, the system shall meteo-normalise via the existing ERA5 regression (regress_out_meteo: u10, v10, blh, wind_speed) and deseasonalise via the existing variants (NOX-003.1 intensity-model residual and harmonic-K1), reusing noxus.signal.index read-only.
REQ-003The decomposition inputs shall be NO₂ + meteo + background only; the CREA benchmark (or any steel-output series) shall never be an input to, or a selection criterion for, any step of the decomposition (anti-p-hacking, NFR-001; enforced by the estimator taking no benchmark argument, mirroring NOX-003.1 fit_intensity_trend).

Change bases & resolutions

REQ-010When decomposing, the system shall compute the variance budget on both change bases: (i) first differences of the series, and (ii) the deseasonalised residual (intensity-model residual / harmonic-K1), so the "variance of changes" is measured on a difference basis and on a deseasonalised basis, not on levels.
REQ-011When decomposing, the system shall run at weekly (no2_cube_w.nc) and daily (no2_cube_d.nc, NOX-006) resolution, reporting each (basis × resolution) combination as its own budget so the reader sees the scale/cadence dependence.

Variance partition

REQ-020When computing the budget, the system shall partition the total variance of the footprint change signal into a sequential/hierarchical budget of four non-negative shares that sum to 1 (within rounding): (a) regional — variance explained by the background-ring change; (b) meteo — the incremental variance explained by adding the ERA5 covariates; (c) steel-local — the incremental variance of the footprint over the background+meteo model; and (d) noise — the remaining unexplained residual.
REQ-021When partitioning, the system shall record the entry order of the nested model (regional → +meteo → +local footprint) and shall report the incremental R² (ΔR²) attributed at each step, so the partition is auditable and its order-dependence is explicit (EDGE-001).
REQ-022When a share estimate is required, the system shall emit a variance-partition table with one row per (basis, resolution) carrying: n, the four shares (regional, meteo, steel-local, noise), the entry-order ΔR² terms, and the steel-local share as the headline quantity.

Implied change-detector ceiling

REQ-030When the steel-local change-variance share f is estimated, the system shall report the implied SNR ceiling for a change detector as ≈ sqrt(f) (the maximum attainable correlation of a change detector with the true steel-activity change), explicitly contrasted with the imported level ceiling ≈ sqrt of the Wen attributable cap (attributable_cap = [0.30, 0.43]).
REQ-031When reporting the ceiling, the system shall state which regime holds — ceiling-bound / physical (steel-local change share ≈ level share) or headroom / method-bound (change share >> level share) — and shall frame the observed near-null (weekly r ≈ 0.07) against that ceiling, without asserting either outcome in advance.

Autocorrelation-aware uncertainty

REQ-040When a share (or the implied ceiling) is reported with a confidence interval, the system shall use an autocorrelation-aware estimate — a moving-block bootstrap that preserves serial dependence, reusing the block-length / bootstrap tooling in noxus/validation/robust.py (NOX-008) — with a configurable draw count and a fixed seed, and shall report the naïve i.i.d. interval alongside so the gap is explicit.

Reporting

REQ-050When reporting, the system shall present the budget as a stacked variance-partition figure (regional / meteo / steel-local / noise, per basis × resolution) and a ceiling-comparison (change ceiling vs level ceiling with CIs), and shall write a findings note stating the physical-vs-method-bound verdict and its implication for NOX-010/011.
REQ-051When the outcome is a confirmed ceiling (no headroom), the system shall report it as a valid finding ("the null is SNR-bound / physical"), not as a failure, and shall not cherry-pick the most favourable basis/resolution without reporting all of them (Morris & Zhang 2019).

CLI / orchestration

REQ-060When the user runs the decomposition, the system shall expose it reproducibly as a noxus subcommand driven by a recorded config (bases, resolutions, bootstrap draws, seed, paths), following the NOX-008 scale-sweep wiring; existing subcommands and their defaults are unchanged.

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001Anti-p-hacking. The decomposition is computed on NO₂ + meteo + background alone; the benchmark is never an input or a selection target; no smoothing/threshold/df is chosen against CREA. The core estimator takes no benchmark argument (mirrors NOX-003.1 fit_intensity_trend), and a guard test asserts this.
NFR-002Autocorrelation-aware. Every CI on a share or ceiling accounts for serial correlation via the moving-block bootstrap (reusing robust.py); naïve i.i.d. intervals are reported only alongside, never alone.
NFR-003Honest null designed-for. Both outcomes — confirmed ceiling (physical) or headroom (method-bound) — are valid, reportable products; the report states which and never buries an unfavourable basis/resolution.
NFR-004Reproducibility & determinism. The whole decomposition reproduces from the committed cube + ERA5 snapshot + recorded config; no live fetch at analysis time; bootstrap uses a fixed seed.
NFR-005Additive. Existing signal/attribution/validation/CLI contracts are unchanged; new code is opt-in.
NFR-006No-publish. NO₂-derived per-basis series are gitignored (data/derived/no2/); the small variance-partition CSV + figures may be committed.
NFR-007Stack conformance. Python ≥3.12; passes uv run pytest + uv run ruff check .; no new dependency (numpy/pandas/xarray/scipy/statsmodels already present; the partition needs none).

Edge cases

EDGE-001The nested partition is order-dependent when regional/meteo/local share variance (collinearity) → the system fixes and records the entry order (regional → meteo → local, REQ-021) and states that a commonality/Shapley alternative would redistribute the shared part; it does not silently pick the order that flatters the steel-local share.
EDGE-002An incremental ΔR² comes out negative (suppression / sampling noise) → the system clamps the reported share to 0, records the raw ΔR², and flags the term, rather than reporting a negative variance share.
EDGE-003The daily series has heavy cloud gaps → first differences are taken across genuine adjacent observations only (no interpolation across gaps); the effective n after gap-aware differencing is recorded; a basis with too few points is labelled low-power, not crashed.
EDGE-004The background ring is degenerate (empty/near-empty at some period) → the regional term reuses the existing attribution GeometryError handling; if the regional model cannot be fit, the budget is reported without the regional split and clearly labelled.
EDGE-005A meteo covariate is missing for part of the series → the meteo term is fit on the jointly-available rows and the covered fraction is recorded; the meteo share is not extrapolated over missing periods.
EDGE-006The steel-local share is near zero within its CI → the implied change ceiling collapses toward 0; the report states the null is SNR-bound rather than reporting a spuriously precise tiny ceiling.

Error states

ERR-001If the weekly cube is missing, the decomposition fails with an actionable message naming noxus grid.
ERR-002If the daily resolution is requested but no2_cube_d.nc is absent, the system fails with an actionable message naming the NOX-006 daily-cube command, rather than silently reporting weekly-only.
ERR-003If the ERA5 snapshot required for the meteo term is missing, the system fails with an actionable message naming the ERA5 ingest command, rather than dropping the meteo split without notice.
ERR-004If a caller attempts to pass a benchmark/output series into the decomposition estimator, the system shall refuse (the estimator exposes no such parameter); this is the runtime face of NFR-001.

Acceptance criteria

AC-001On a planted-decomposition synthetic signal (footprint_change = regional + β·meteo + local + noise with known variance shares), the partition recovers the four shares within tolerance and they sum to ≈ 1 (REQ-020/021).
AC-002Anti-p-hacking guard: the core decomposition function takes no benchmark argument (asserted by signature inspection), and the module never imports/reads the CREA benchmark (REQ-003, NFR-001, ERR-004).
AC-003The budget is emitted on both change bases (first differences, deseasonalised residual) at both resolutions (weekly, daily), one table row each (REQ-010/011/022).
AC-004The implied change-detector ceiling ≈ sqrt(steel-local share) is reported and contrasted with the level ceiling ≈ sqrt(attributable_cap); the physical-vs-method-bound verdict is stated (REQ-030/031).
AC-005The CI on the steel-local share (and the ceiling) uses the moving-block bootstrap and is wider than the naïve i.i.d. interval on an autocorrelated fixture; both are reported (REQ-040, NFR-002).
AC-006A negative incremental ΔR² is clamped to 0, the raw value recorded and the term flagged (EDGE-002); daily gap-aware differencing records the effective n (EDGE-003).
AC-007The stacked variance-partition figure + ceiling-comparison + findings note are produced; a confirmed ceiling is framed as a valid finding; no basis/resolution is cherry-picked (REQ-050/051, NFR-003).
AC-008Re-running with the same seed/config reproduces the table identically (NFR-004); existing signal/attribution/validation/CLI behaviour is unchanged (NFR-005); uv run pytest and uv run ruff check . pass (NFR-007).

Memory and decision implications

Likely decision-log candidates (proposed at review, not pre-written): (1) adopting the measured change-variance share — not the imported Wen mean-level share — as the SNR-ceiling basis for change/activity detectors going forward; (2) the empirical verdict on whether the Tangshan near-null is SNR-bound (physical) or has headroom (method/target-bound), which sets the go/no-go rationale for NOX-010 (deweathering) and NOX-011 (VIIRS triangulation). Both are methodological choices future sessions should not re-litigate.

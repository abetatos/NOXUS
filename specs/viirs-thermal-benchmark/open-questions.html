Q1Is the keyless VNF path usable? → RESOLVED: VNF dropped (not public)Resolved
Question
What is the exact VNF (EOG / CSM) download endpoint and per-night file format, does it require authentication, and is the keyless/free path usable for a reproducible pipeline?
Why it matters
Drives the acquisition client and whether VNF can be the primary series under the project's public/free reproducibility principle.
Blocking
No
Resolution (2026-08-22, developer-directed pivot)
VNF is DROPPED — it is no longer public. A VIIRS Nightfire Data Use License (effective 2025-01-10) gates the data, and EOG limits programmatic (OpenID/OAuth) access to paid subscribers (effective 2026-06-01); a free EOG account gives browser-only access, which is not automatable or reproducible. This conflicts with the project's public/free reproducible-data principle, so VNF is out of scope. (Earlier 2026-07-12 finding: the defaulted bulk path returned a Keycloak SSO login page — VnfFetcher raised ViirsAuthError; the licence + pay-gate confirm the path cannot be made keyless/reproducible.) The VNF code path now raises a clear licence/pay-gated error (ERR-006).

Q7FIRMS signal is sparse / low-FRP — is there enough statistical power? → RESOLVED: proceed, honest null acceptedResolved
Question
The FIRMS active-fire signal over the plants is sparse (many zero-detection nights) and low-FRP (0.2–4.8 MW). Is there enough statistical power for the NO₂↔VIIRS / CREA↔VIIRS triangulation to distinguish the feature-vs-target branches?
Why it matters
Low power could leave the triangulation inconclusive. It must not be spun as a positive or a failure.
Blocking
No
Default / resolution (2026-08-22)
Proceed. A weak or null result is a first-class, valid outcome (Morris & Zhang 2019 honest-null; consistent with NOX-008/009). The weekly series zero-fills and flags sparse bins, and the triangulation surfaces the power caveat explicitly (n_eff, zero-week fraction, FRP range; REQ-025). No result is massaged toward significance. The triangulation logic is unchanged by the pivot.

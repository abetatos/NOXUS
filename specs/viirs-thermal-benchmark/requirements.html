Summary

Acquire an independent, physical activity proxy for the Tangshan steel cluster — VIIRS thermal anomalies over the catalogued steel facilities — and use it to run the decisive triangulation that breaks the feature-vs-target ambiguity raised by critique #1 (the “fraudulent / unverifiable target”). Blast furnaces, sinter plants and coke ovens are high-temperature combustion sources that VIIRS detects from orbit; a space-based thermometer is hard to falsify and cannot be dressed up by any statistical office (“maquillaje”). The CREA benchmark is a commercial trader survey (WIND / Custeel), not official statistics — its provenance is not independently verifiable — so an orthogonal physical proxy is the one remaining lever to localise where the near-null NO₂↔steel result comes from.

The decisive logic (state explicitly). Build a VIIRS thermal series over the facilities and run two correlations under the NOX-008 autocorrelation-robust significance: (i) NO₂ ↔ VIIRS and (ii) CREA ↔ VIIRS. Interpretation:
• If NO₂ does not even track the physical heat of the furnaces → the FEATURE (NO₂ at this scale) is the weak link — consistent with NOX-009's finding that steel-local NO₂ change-variance is ≈0 (SNR-bound).
• If NO₂↔VIIRS holds but CREA↔VIIRS does not → the TARGET (CREA) was the weak link, retroactively re-interpreting every prior near-null.
• If VIIRS↔CREA holds but NO₂ tracks neither → confirms NO₂ is blind at this scale while the target is sound. Every branch is informative: an honest null localises the weak link either way (Morris & Zhang 2019).

This is a new-acquisition task in the style of NOX-002a (TROPOMI): public/free data only, credentials by environment-variable reference only, a resumable manifest, and VIIRS-derived raw outputs gitignored (small stats + figures committable). It is the heaviest task on the roadmap for new data and is independent of NOX-009/010.

Product (given as the factual basis; access details are unknowns resolved by the T1 spike). The primary thermal series is VIIRS Nightfire (VNF) from the NOAA / Colorado School of Mines Earth Observation Group (EOG, Payne Institute): it detects sub-pixel high-temperature sources at night across multiple near/short-wave IR bands and reports per-detection temperature (K), source area and radiant heat (MW) — physically matched to industrial combustion (steel, gas flares). It is nightly, ~2012→present, global, free bulk download (per-night CSV, filtered to the AOI client-side); night-only is acceptable because steel is 24/7 baseload. As an independent cross-check, NASA FIRMS VIIRS active fire (VNP14IMGT S-NPP + VJ114 NOAA-20, 375 m, free, MAP_KEY by email) is active-fire-tuned but its static-land-source detections capture persistent industrial hot spots. See assumptions.html (A1) and open-questions.html (Q1/Q2).

Gate. A T1 feasibility spike (like GEMS/NOX-007) precedes the full build: fetch a small VNF window over Tangshan, confirm detections land on the facilities, measure per-night volume + available fields + temporal coverage, and record a go/no-go. This resolves the external-access unknowns before the acquisition loop is written (REQ-000).

Functional requirements

EARS. IDs group by stage: 000 gate, 001–009 acquisition, 010–019 join + thermal series, 020–029 triangulation, 030–039 verification/output, 040+ CLI.

Viability gate (spike)

REQ-000Before building the acquisition loop, the system shall (T1 spike, developer-run) fetch a small VNF window over Tangshan and record: (a) the download endpoint + file format and whether authentication is required; (b) per-night data volume and the implied full-era volume; (c) the detection fields available (temperature K, source area, radiant heat MW, lat/lon, timestamp, detection id, cloud/quality flags); (d) temporal coverage over the AOI (nights with ≥1 detection vs gaps); (e) confirmation that detections spatially co-locate with the catalogued facilities. A go/no-go against a download-budget threshold shall be recorded in open-questions.html (Q1) and the decision log before further implementation.

Acquisition

REQ-001When acquisition runs, the system shall retrieve VNF thermal detections over the Tangshan AOI for the configured date range (default: the TROPOMI/CREA overlap era), filtering each per-night product to the AOI bbox client-side and retaining detections inside it.
REQ-002When acquiring, the system shall retain, per detection: temperature (K), source area, radiant heat (MW), latitude/longitude, observation timestamp, a detection id, and any available quality/cloud flag.
REQ-003When a night is ingested, the system shall persist the AOI-clipped detections reproducibly under data/raw/viirs/, tagged with date, AOI bbox, product/source, and fetch time (provenance).
REQ-004While acquiring the record, the system shall be idempotent and resumable: a manifest records fetched nights; re-running skips completed nights and continues after interruption; acquisition shall support batching by date window with retry/backoff.
REQ-005Where the chosen source requires credentials (e.g. FIRMS MAP_KEY), the system shall read them from an environment variable via .env (per .env.example), referencing the variable name only — never reading, logging, printing, or committing the value. VNF is expected to need no auth (confirmed at T1).
REQ-006The system shall additionally support an independent FIRMS active-fire cross-check acquisition (VNP14IMGT + VJ114) over the same AOI/era, retaining only static-land-source detections (the FIRMS detection type field), used as a robustness series — not the primary.

Facility join + thermal series

REQ-010When building the thermal series, the system shall join detections to the catalogued steel facilities (data/derived/tangshan_steel_facilities.csv) by assigning each detection to a facility whose centroid lies within a fixed facility radius (config default; A2), discarding detections outside every facility radius.
REQ-011When aggregating, the system shall produce a weekly (primary) and daily (secondary) thermal-activity series over the joined detections, with the primary aggregate = summed radiant heat (MW) and alternates = detection count and mean detection temperature retained alongside (A3).
REQ-012The thermal series shall be constructed benchmark-blind: the facility radius, quality thresholds, aggregation frequency and primary aggregate are fixed and recorded before the triangulation outputs are viewed (anti-p-hacking; A9, NFR-006). The series must not reference the CREA benchmark or the NO₂ result in its construction.

Triangulation

REQ-020When triangulation runs, the system shall compute NO₂-footprint ↔ VIIRS correlation (Pearson r + lead/lag) under the NOX-008 autocorrelation-robust significance by reusing noxus/validation/robust.py (robust_corr) — effective-N, Newey-West, moving-block bootstrap CI, block-permutation null. It shall not re-implement significance.
REQ-021When triangulation runs, the system shall compute CREA ↔ VIIRS correlation (Pearson r + lead/lag) under the same robust significance, on the common overlap window.
REQ-022The system shall apply Benjamini-Hochberg FDR (bh_fdr) across the family of correlation p-values (NO₂↔VIIRS, CREA↔VIIRS, and the FIRMS cross-check pair) so the multiplicity is corrected transparently.
REQ-023The system shall emit an interpretation / decision table that maps the observed pattern of significant links onto the feature-vs-target diagnosis (NO₂ blind → feature is the weak link; CREA↔VIIRS absent while NO₂↔VIIRS holds → target was the weak link; VIIRS↔CREA holds while NO₂ tracks neither → NO₂ blind, target sound), including the honest-null branch.
REQ-024The system shall repeat the triangulation on the FIRMS static-land-source series as an independent robustness check, reporting whether the diagnosis is consistent across the two VIIRS products (REQ-006).

Verification + output

REQ-030Verification shall render the VIIRS detections over the AOI with the steel-facility locations overlaid, so a reviewer can confirm the thermal detections co-locate with the steel facilities (reusing the facilities CSV + overlay convention).
REQ-031The system shall emit small, committable outputs under data/derived/: the thermal-activity series and the triangulation result table (CSV/parquet + figures). VIIRS-derived raw detections stay gitignored (NFR-003).

CLI

REQ-040When the user runs acquisition, series-build, triangulation and verification via the noxus CLI, the system shall expose them as documented subcommands (e.g. fetch-viirs with a --probe spike mode, build-thermal-series, triangulate-thermal, verify-viirs) driven by noxus/config/ (AOI/buffer, date range, source, facility radius, quality thresholds, frequency, budget).

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001Reproducibility. Acquisition, join and triangulation are parameterised and provenance-tagged; given the same config and source state, the kept dataset and the triangulation table are reproducible (fixed RNG seeds via robust_corr).
NFR-002Public/free + credentials by reference. Uses only free VNF (EOG) and FIRMS. Any key (e.g. FIRMS_MAP_KEY) is read from .env per .env.example; the variable name is referenced, the value never read into logs/output or committed.
NFR-003Storage discipline. VIIRS-derived raw detections live under gitignored data/raw/viirs/; only small derived stats + figures are committable (data/derived/).
NFR-004Stack conformance. Python ≥3.12; passes uv run ruff check . and uv run pytest; downloads via the existing httpx stack; the facility-radius join uses a numpy haversine (no new geospatial dependency). No new dependency shall be added without developer approval (Q5).
NFR-005Honest null is designed-for and informative. A null (no significant correlation) is a valid, reportable outcome; the value of this task is that every branch localises the weak link (see the decisive logic). No result is massaged to reach significance.
NFR-006Anti-p-hacking / pre-registration. The facility radius, quality thresholds, aggregation frequency and primary aggregate are fixed and recorded (in assumptions.html) before the triangulation is viewed; the thermal series is built benchmark-blind (REQ-012).
NFR-007Politeness. Downloads are rate-limited / sequential with retry+backoff to respect the free services; the run is resumable so it can span sessions.
NFR-008Additive. No change to existing TROPOMI/ERA5/benchmark/attribution behaviour; VIIRS is a parallel acquisition + analysis channel. Existing modules and outputs are untouched.

Edge cases

EDGE-001A night with no detections over the AOI (cloud, no overpass, or genuinely no hot spot) → recorded as zero-activity for that night, not an error and not interpolated (honest gaps, same policy as TROPOMI coverage).
EDGE-002A detection falls within the radius of more than one facility (facilities are dense) → assigned to the nearest facility centroid; a detection outside every radius is dropped from the facility series (REQ-010).
EDGE-003The VIIRS overlap era is shorter than the CREA / NO₂ series → triangulation aligns on the common window; effective-N shrinks accordingly and is reported (never padded).
EDGE-004FIRMS returns detections tagged as presumed vegetation fire / offshore / volcano rather than static land source → filtered out for the industrial cross-check (REQ-006), keeping only static-land-source.
EDGE-005A weekly bin has very few detection-nights (sparse thermal sampling) → the aggregate is still computed but the sparse-coverage weeks are flagged so downstream can weight/exclude them.
EDGE-006The two VIIRS products (VNF vs FIRMS) disagree in coverage (different overpass/detection logic) → each is aggregated independently; the triangulation reports both, and REQ-024 flags any diagnosis divergence rather than blending them.

Error states

ERR-001If a required credential (e.g. FIRMS_MAP_KEY) is missing/invalid, the system shall fail with a clear message referencing the environment-variable name — never echoing the value.
ERR-002If the download endpoint returns an error / empty listing for a date range, or the expected detection fields are absent from a product, the system shall raise a descriptive error naming the source/date/field attempted.
ERR-003If a download fails irrecoverably for a batch, the system shall record the failure, leave already-fetched nights intact, and allow a resume (REQ-004).
ERR-004If the measured per-night / full-era download volume (REQ-000) exceeds the configured budget threshold, the system shall stop and surface the no-go rather than silently starting an oversized download.
ERR-005If the facilities CSV is missing or malformed, the join step shall raise a descriptive error rather than silently producing an empty series.

Acceptance criteria

AC-000The T1 spike produces a recorded measurement of the VNF endpoint/format, auth requirement, per-night volume, available fields, AOI temporal coverage, and facility co-location — plus a written go/no-go against the budget.
AC-001A small date-range acquisition over the AOI returns AOI-clipped VNF detections with the required fields + provenance, and re-running skips completed nights (idempotent/resumable), keeping raw detections gitignored.
AC-002The join + aggregation produces a weekly (primary) and daily (secondary) thermal-activity series over the facilities (summed radiant heat primary; count + mean-temperature alternates), constructed benchmark-blind, with sparse-coverage weeks flagged.
AC-003The triangulation emits NO₂↔VIIRS and CREA↔VIIRS robust_corr results (r, effective-N, bootstrap CI, permutation p) with BH-FDR across the family and an interpretation table covering the honest-null branch — reusing robust.py, not re-implementing significance.
AC-004The FIRMS static-land-source cross-check runs the same triangulation and the report states whether the diagnosis is consistent across the two products.
AC-005Verification renders VIIRS detections over the AOI with facility points overlaid (co-location check); committable series + triangulation table + figures land under data/derived/.
AC-006uv run pytest and uv run ruff check . pass; all network/auth is mocked in tests (synthetic detection fixtures, no network).

External dependencies and APIs

  • VIIRS Nightfire (VNF) — NOAA / Colorado School of Mines Earth Observation Group (EOG, Payne Institute, mines.edu). Per-detection temperature (K), source area, radiant heat (MW); nightly, ~2012→present, global, free bulk per-night files. Primary series. Exact endpoint/format/auth resolved at T1 (Q1).
  • NASA FIRMS VIIRS active fire — VNP14IMGT (S-NPP) + VJ114 (NOAA-20), 375 m, free, area+date API and archive; MAP_KEY by email (env var FIRMS_MAP_KEY). Detection type field distinguishes static land source from vegetation fire. Cross-check series (Q2).
  • Facilities CSV — data/derived/tangshan_steel_facilities.csv (join target + overlay). Note: the committed CSV is the source of truth for the facility set (Q6).
  • AOI — noxus.config.region.TANGSHAN / tangshan_aoi(buffer) (reused).
  • NO₂ footprint series + CREA benchmark series — existing derived artifacts (the two series triangulated against VIIRS).
  • Significance — noxus/validation/robust.py (robust_corr, bh_fdr) and noxus/validation/leadlag.py, reused unchanged.

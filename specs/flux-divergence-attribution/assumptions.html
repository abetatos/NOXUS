Assumptions

A1 Raw L2 __NO2___ swath is available free via CDSE OData/S3 Pending
Assumption
The native-footprint L2 S5P_*_L2__NO2___ product needed for fine oversampling is retrievable from the free CDSE OData catalogue (Bearer token) or S3 eodata endpoint, with the averaging kernel + geolocation readable per granule, reusing the NOX-002a CDSE credentials.
Reason
docs/data-access.md already documents the CDSE OData route and S5P_*_L2__NO2___ products; NOX-002a proved the CDSE token flow. openEO (NOX-002a) delivered only the gridded product, so raw L2 is genuinely new but on the same free platform.
Risk level
Medium
Impact if wrong
If the raw-L2 read path differs materially (kernel/geolocation access, product naming), the fetch module needs adjustment; verified cheaply at T1 before the full fetch.
Blocks implementation
No — verified at T1 (Q1).

A2 Effective wind = ERA5 10 m (with a boundary-layer-mean option) Pending
Assumption
The transport wind defaults to the existing ERA5 u10/v10, with a configurable boundary-layer-mean option (using blh); the default is fixed pre-outcome and its effect reported as a sensitivity (REQ-006), not tuned.
Reason
ERA5 is already ingested (NOX-003/010); a boundary-layer-representative wind is the Beirle recommendation, and 10 m is the available, reproducible default.
Risk level
Medium
Impact if wrong
Wrong wind level biases the flux; mitigated by reporting sensitivity across levels (EDGE-004) and by the T1 co-location check.

A3 Lifetime τ = a fixed literature central value + sensitivity range Pending
Assumption
The chemical-loss term uses a fixed literature NOx lifetime τ (order a few hours, seasonally aware) with a documented NO2:NOx ratio, reported across a sensitivity range (EDGE-005), not a single value tuned to CREA.
Reason
Beirle et al. use an effective-lifetime loss term; a fixed literature value keeps the build benchmark-blind and reproducible.
Risk level
Medium
Impact if wrong
τ scales the loss correction; mitigated by the sensitivity sweep and by the fact that the divergence term (not the sink) carries the localisation.
Blocks implementation
No

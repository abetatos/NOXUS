Requirements — Flux-divergence point-source attribution

Task ID NOX-005
Feature slug flux-divergence-attribution
Status spec_draft
Author spec-author
Human approvalpending

Summary

Attribute a Tangshan steel-cluster NOx emission signal directly, using the NO2 flux-divergence method (Beirle et al., Science 2019; 2021; 2023). The flux is the tropospheric NO2 vertical column density (TVCD) transported by the horizontal wind, F = VNO2 · u; the horizontal divergence ∇·F isolates the local source term by cancelling the smoothly advected regional background, with a chemical-loss correction (an effective NOx decay term set by the lifetime τ) and plume-height / effective-wind handling. The output is a benchmark-blind, facility-attributed steel source series, tested against the CREA physical-output benchmark only after it is built.

This is the only remaining method in the roadmap that directly attacks the project's root cause. Four convergent honest-nulls precede it:

  • NOX-009 measured 93–95% of NO2 change-variance as regional common-mode and the Tangshan steel-local share at ≈0; the current relative-index / deweathering pipeline therefore cannot separate the steel-local signal.
  • NOX-011 (VIIRS thermal), NOX-012 (curtailment-calendar regime study), and NOX-013 (nowcast) each returned an honest null on the steel-local separation.

Flux-divergence is the explicit DL-A revisit condition: "a source-separation method that demonstrably lifts the steel-local change-variance share materially above 0" (decisions/rejected-options.md, 2026-08-27). A null here would be decisive: even the best public point-source method cannot separate Tangshan from the North China Plain background. The task's own definition warns the method "resolves poorly in high-background / complex-wind regions like industrial NE China — expected to be hard at Tangshan." An honest null is therefore an anticipated, first-class outcome, and the build is gated behind a feasibility spike (T1) that decides on evidence whether a coherent local source term is recoverable at all.

Honest feasibility caveat (developer decision input). Two facts frame this task before approval. (1) The method is known to be hard in exactly this regime (high background, complex NE-China winds), so a NO-GO at T1 is a realistic outcome. (2) Fine-grid oversampling + a divergence operator over a multi-year raw L2 swath record is compute- and storage-heavy (see NFR-008 and the design's compute profile). The developer should weigh both before approving implementation; the T1 gate exists precisely to make the go/no-go call cheaply, on a bounded data subset, before the full pipeline is built.

Functional requirements

EARS format. IDs are stable references for design, tasks, and tests.

REQ-001 The system shall acquire raw Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI L2 __NO2___ swath granules over the Tangshan AOI from a free, public source — the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE) OData catalogue (S5P_*_L2__NO2___, OFFL + RPRO for a version-consistent record), with the S3 eodata endpoint as a high-throughput alternative — because the existing openEO product (NOX-002a) is gridded and lacks the native footprints needed for fine oversampling. The fetch shall be resumable/idempotent via a manifest.
REQ-002 The system shall oversample the L2 TVCD (with its averaging kernel and QA screening, qa_value ≥ 0.75 default) onto a fine regular grid over the AOI, at a pre-registered target resolution, accumulating multiple overpasses per analysis period to build a dense mean-column field.
REQ-003 The system shall compute the horizontal NO2 flux field F = VNO2 · u by combining the oversampled TVCD with a boundary-layer-representative (effective) horizontal wind derived from ERA5 (reusing the existing ERA5 ingest: u10/v10, and blh where a boundary-layer-mean wind is used), sampled at the swath overpass time.
REQ-004 The system shall compute the horizontal divergence ∇·F of the flux field, isolating the local emission source term by cancelling the smoothly advected regional background.
REQ-005 The system shall apply a chemical-loss correction: an effective NOx sink term VNO2 / τ (with a configurable, literature-based lifetime τ and its NO2:NOx ratio), so that the recovered source term E = ∇·F + VNO2/τ approximates the emission rate rather than the raw divergence.
REQ-006 The system shall treat plume-height / effective-wind as an explicit, configurable modelling choice (wind level or boundary-layer-mean, with a documented default), and shall report the source term's sensitivity to that choice rather than silently fixing it (EDGE-004).
REQ-007 The system shall compute the divergence and source-term field benchmark-blind: no step that builds the field may read, be fit to, or be tuned against the CREA benchmark or any market series. All operator parameters (resolution, τ, wind level, QA/coverage thresholds) shall be fixed as pre-registered defaults before any benchmark statistic is viewed.
REQ-008 The system shall attribute the source-term field to the steel facilities: integrate/aggregate the recovered emission field over the committed Tangshan steel-facility footprints (GEM Global Iron & Steel Tracker; data/derived/tangshan_steel_facilities.csv), reusing the existing regional-background subtraction where applicable, to produce a Tangshan steel NOx source time series at the configured analysis frequency (weekly default).
REQ-009 After the source series is built, the system shall run the correlation/lead test against CREA reusing the NOX-008 autocorrelation-robust machinery byte-for-byte (robust_corr, block_permutation_p, bh_fdr, block_length; lead_lag) and the weekly-resample-then-align convention (align_series), never an exact-date join (the NOX-012 B3 bug). This comparison happens only after the blind field is built; it is never fit to.
REQ-010 The system shall report an honest null as a first-class outcome: if no coherent local source term is recoverable, or the attributed steel source does not lift the steel-local signal materially above the regional background, or it shows no significant relationship to CREA, the report shall state that plainly — "even the best public point-source method cannot separate Tangshan from the North China Plain background" — without re-tuning any parameter to manufacture a positive result.
REQ-011 Every output shall state the scale caveat: Tangshan is ~13% of national steel, the attribution is a cluster signal (not a national estimate), and Hebei-provincial statistics are preferred as a physical cross-check where available.
REQ-012 The system shall expose the raw-L2 fetch, the flux-divergence attribution, and its diagnostics via the existing noxus CLI pattern (config-driven, resumable where a fetch is involved), following the conventions of the existing subcommands, as new additive commands that do not alter the existing relative-index pipeline.
REQ-013 The system shall write raw swath granules and derived high-resolution fields to gitignored derived/raw paths (large EO downloads, per project convention), and shall emit small committable summary statistics and figures (the source-term map over the facilities, the attributed source series, the CREA comparison table, the T1 verdict).
REQ-014 The system shall surface the compute and storage profile: report the expected raw-swath volume, oversampling/divergence cost, and whether the full multi-year run is laptop-feasible or server-class (rough order of magnitude), and shall support running on a bounded data subset (a short date range / reduced extent) so the T1 gate and early tasks execute cheaply before any full-record commitment.
REQ-015 Before the full attribution pipeline is built, the system shall run a feasibility spike (T1) — a coarse, benchmark-blind go/no-go — on a bounded data subset, assessing whether the divergence field produces a coherent, physically-plausible, spatially-localised source term over the steel cluster at all (does a divergence/source hotspot co-locate with known plants; is the source signal above the field noise), and shall record a three-way verdict before any downstream task runs: GO (coherent source field → build full attribution), GO-degraded (partial/noisy but usable → build with caveats), or NO-GO (no coherent local source recoverable → honest null; stop, escalate to the developer).

Non-functional requirements

NFR-001 Public/free data only. Every input (raw L2 swath, ERA5 wind, facility footprints, benchmark) shall be public/free; the raw-L2 path shall be the free CDSE OData/S3 route. No paid or licence-gated data source shall be introduced (the NOX-011 VNF lesson). Credentials shall be referenced by environment-variable name only, never read or logged.
NFR-002 Anti-p-hacking. The divergence/source field is computed benchmark-blind (REQ-007). All operator parameters shall be fixed as defaults before any CREA statistic is viewed; no parameter (resolution, τ, wind level, thresholds) may be selected to maximise the reported correlation. The honest null (REQ-010) is first-class.
NFR-003 Reuse, do not reimplement. The build shall reuse existing artefacts: the ERA5 ingest (NOX-003/010, noxus/data/era5.py), the AOI + facility footprints (noxus/config/region.py, the facilities CSV), the regional-background subtraction (noxus/attribution/source.py), the openEO acquisition patterns (NOX-002a), and the NOX-008 statistics + alignment modules. Only the raw-L2 fetch, oversampling, and divergence operator are genuinely new.
NFR-004 Autocorrelation-robust significance. Any correlation of the attributed source series vs CREA shall reuse noxus/validation/robust.py (robust_corr, block_permutation_p, bh_fdr, block_length) and leadlag.py (lead_lag) byte-for-byte, using the weekly-resample-then-align convention (align_series), never an exact-date join.
NFR-005 Scale caveat explicit. Every output states Tangshan ≈13% of national steel and prefers Hebei-provincial statistics where available (REQ-011).
NFR-006 Determinism. Any bootstrap/permutation inference shall use a fixed seed; the seed and all fixed operator defaults (resolution, τ, wind level, thresholds, QA) shall be recorded in the output provenance.
NFR-007 Additive, non-breaking. The flux-divergence attribution shall be a new additive stage (package + config + CLI command(s)); it shall not alter the behaviour of the existing relative-index pipeline, CLI commands, or reused modules.
NFR-008 Compute/storage profile documented. The design shall state the expected raw-swath storage, oversampling/divergence compute cost (order of magnitude), and the laptop-vs-server recommendation, so the developer can make an informed resource decision at approval (REQ-014). The full record shall never be a prerequisite for the T1 gate.

Edge cases

EDGE-001 High-background / complex-wind regime (the expected-hard case). When the divergence is dominated by advected regional background and no localised source term rises above the field noise over the facilities, the system shall report it (T1 → GO-degraded or NO-GO) and shall not fabricate a source hotspot.
EDGE-002 Coverage / cloud gaps. Periods with insufficient valid oversampled coverage (too few clear high-QA overpasses) yield NaN/marked source values, never interpolated or fabricated fields.
EDGE-003 Calm-wind / stagnation days. When the wind speed is below a configured threshold the flux ≈ 0 and the divergence is ill-conditioned; such overpasses are down-weighted or excluded per the threshold and flagged, not silently included.
EDGE-004 Wind-level / plume-height mismatch. The 10 m wind may not represent the plume-transport level; the system reports the source term's sensitivity across the configured wind-level choices rather than presenting a single value as exact (REQ-006).
EDGE-005 Lifetime τ uncertainty. τ varies with season and NOx regime; the loss correction is reported across a τ sensitivity range, not a single tuned value (REQ-005, NFR-002).
EDGE-006 Series too short for robust inference. After alignment, if the source series is too short for autocorrelation-robust inference, the system reports insufficient power rather than a spuriously significant result.
EDGE-007 Grid too fine for the data density. Over-fine oversampling injects noise; the target resolution is a fixed pre-registered default (NFR-002), never tuned to maximise the CREA correlation.

Error states

ERR-001 If the raw L2 swath store is absent, the system shall raise a clear error naming the missing input and the fetch command that produces it, and shall not emit an attribution.
ERR-002 If the T1 feasibility spike returns NO-GO (no coherent local source term recoverable on the bounded subset at the fixed defaults), the system shall stop the build and record the honest-null verdict; downstream tasks shall not run.
ERR-003 If the ERA5 wind store is missing or cannot be aligned to the swath overpass times, the system shall raise a clear error (naming ingest-era5) and shall not silently fill or assume a wind field.
ERR-004 If configuration is invalid (e.g. non-positive τ, non-positive wind threshold, resolution finer than the data supports, unknown wind level, analysis frequency finer than the swath cadence supports), the system shall raise a validation error before any computation.
ERR-005 If a paid or licence-gated data source is requested for any input, the system shall refuse it (public/free only, NFR-001), directing the user to the free CDSE route.
ERR-006 If the benchmark-blind guard detects that the source-field builder was passed CREA (or market) values, the system shall fail loudly (test-time), never shipping a benchmark-fit field.

Acceptance criteria

AC-001 The raw-L2 fetch acquires S5P_*_L2__NO2___ swath granules over the AOI from the free CDSE OData/S3 route, resumable via a manifest, into a gitignored store, with provenance recording product/processor version. (REQ-001, REQ-012, REQ-013, NFR-001)
AC-002 Oversampling → flux → divergence → chemical-loss correction produce a source-term field with provenance recording resolution, τ (+ range), wind level, QA/coverage thresholds, and seed. (REQ-002..006, NFR-006)
AC-003 A benchmark-blind guard test proves the source-field builder takes no CREA/market argument and reads no benchmark values. (REQ-007, NFR-002, ERR-006)
AC-004 Facility attribution over the committed footprints yields a Tangshan steel NOx source time series at the configured frequency. (REQ-008)
AC-005 The CREA comparison uses the NOX-008 robust modules byte-for-byte and the weekly-resample-then-align convention (not an exact-date join), reporting robust r/p, BH-FDR, and lead/lag. (REQ-009, NFR-004)
AC-006 When no coherent/lifting source is found, the report states an honest null and no parameter is re-tuned to manufacture an edge. (REQ-010, NFR-002)
AC-007 The T1 spike records a three-way verdict (GO / GO-degraded / NO-GO) with its evidence (facility co-location + signal-vs-noise) on a bounded subset before any downstream task runs; a NO-GO stops the build. (REQ-015, ERR-002)
AC-008 The compute/storage profile is estimated and surfaced, a bounded-subset run is supported, and the scale caveat (Tangshan ≈13%, Hebei preferred) appears in every output. (REQ-011, REQ-014, NFR-005, NFR-008)

External dependencies and APIs

  • CDSE raw L2 __NO2___ swath — free, via the OData catalogue (catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1) with a Bearer token, or the S3 eodata.dataspace.copernicus.eu endpoint; credentials by CDSE_* name only. This is the new acquisition path (NOX-002a delivered only the gridded openEO product). Exact per-variable read path (averaging kernel, geolocation) is verified at T1 — see open-questions.html Q1.
  • ERA5 winds (u10/v10, blh) — already ingested (NOX-003/010, noxus ingest-era5, Copernicus CDS); the transport field for the flux.
  • Steel-facility footprints — committed (data/derived/tangshan_steel_facilities.csv, GEM Global Iron & Steel Tracker); the attribution targets.
  • CREA / Mysteel / WIND BF operating rate — already ingested (NOX-001); the post-hoc benchmark, never fit to.

Verification evidence (sources, dates, versions) belongs in the design's External dependencies and freshness section, filled at/after T1.

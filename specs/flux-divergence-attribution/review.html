Validity of the powered-null argument (the crux)

The claim is: “noise fell 3.4× while SNR stayed flat → no separable source.” I checked whether this could be a bug that moves numerator and denominator together mechanically.

  • Numerator and denominator are independent statistics. In _coherence_from_e (probe.py:216-221) the numerator is median(E[footprint]) − median(E[background]) — a location contrast — while the denominator is 1.4826 × MAD(E[background]) — a dispersion of a different quantity. They are not algebraically linked. A localized persistent source raises the footprint median while the background dispersion falls under oversampling, so SNR climbs ~√N. A pure-noise field has a numerator that is itself a noise-scale quantity, so it falls in lockstep with the denominator and SNR stays flat. This is exactly the observed behaviour.
  • Averaging genuinely reduces noise. _mean_source_field (probe.py:273-302) averages the per-overpass flux fields (each with its own ERA5 wind) via nanmean, then takes the divergence once. Divergence is linear, so independent per-overpass noise in the mean flux falls ~1/√N in E. The measured curve confirms it (noise 3.62e-08 → 1.07e-08 at N=4, even faster than 1/√N as coverage gaps also fill).
  • A source-present control exists and genuinely discriminates. test_multi_overpass_oversampling_separates_source_as_noise_falls (test_probe.py:177-193) plants a real co-located Gaussian source over 8 synthetic overpasses with winds spread around the circle and asserts both that noise falls (curve[-1].e_noise < curve[0].e_noise) and that SNR rises (curve[-1].snr > curve[0].snr). Its twin test_multi_overpass_background_only_stays_flat_and_powers_no_go (test_probe.py:196-208) uses the same machinery with no bump and asserts a flat, NO-GO outcome. The pair confirms the diagnostic distinguishes source-present from source-absent — so the real-data flat SNR is a true null, not an artefact.

Conclusion: the powered-null inference is valid. The N=4 stop is honestly hedged in the report (“bounded budget”) and is defensible because SNR scales only ~√N and is already flat while noise fell 3.4×.
